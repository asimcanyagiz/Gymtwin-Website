Privacy Policy

What Gymtwin collects, what stays on your iPhone, and how to delete it.

Effective date: October 22, 2026

The short version

  • Height, weight, body fat, sex and date of birth read from Apple Health stay on your iPhone.
  • Your workouts, check-ins, plan and any values you type in are backed up under a random account ID. We don't ask for your name or email address.
  • No ads, no tracking across apps and websites, no advertising identifier (IDFA), and we never sell your data.
  • Delete my data in the app erases your account and progress from our servers and from your phone.

Who we are

This policy covers the Gymtwin app for iPhone ("Gymtwin", "the app") and this website. Gymtwin is developed and published by Asım Can Yağız ("we", "us"), who decides how personal data in the app is used and is the data controller. You can reach us at apputopiacy@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account identifier

When you first open Gymtwin, the app creates an anonymous account with Google Firebase Authentication. It is identified by a random user ID. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.

If you choose Back up with Apple, we link your Sign in with Apple identity to the same account so you can restore your progress on another iPhone. Apple gives us a unique identifier for your Apple Account. Gymtwin does not ask Apple for your name or email address.

Your progress

To keep your streak, XP and level safe, the app stores these records on our servers under your account, whether or not you back up with Apple:

  • Workouts you save: start time, duration, the body regions you trained, how hard it felt, whether the time came from a live workout, Apple Health or manual entry, and any exercises with sets, weight and reps. If the workout was pre-filled from an Apple Health workout, we also store that Health workout's identifier so the same workout never counts twice.
  • Rest-day check-ins, and a note when you remove a workout on the same day you logged it.
  • Your training plan (the weekdays you train), your goal and your training experience.
  • Your twin's look, name and starting level, and progress bonuses the app grants.
  • When your subscription became active or inactive, so the app can pause your streak while you're not subscribed.
  • Your time zone and the day each record belongs to, plus when your account was created and last synced.

Backing up with Apple is what lets you get these records back on a new phone. Without it, they stay tied to the anonymous account on this phone.

Body measurements you enter yourself

If you type in your height, weight, body fat percentage, sex or birth year in Gymtwin, rather than reading them from Apple Health, those values are stored with your progress.

Device identifier

The app creates a random identifier when it's installed and attaches it to each record, so we can tell which of your devices wrote it. It is not the advertising identifier (IDFA) and it is never used for tracking.

Crash data

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report with technical details about the crash and your device, such as the device model, iOS version, app version and an identifier for the app installation. We don't attach your user ID, your health or fitness data, or custom logs to crash reports.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your Gymtwin user ID, your App Store purchase information (such as the product, purchase and renewal dates, and transaction identifiers) and technical data its software sends with each request (such as IP address, App Store country, app version and device model). We don't send RevenueCat your name, email address or any health or fitness data. We never see your payment card details.

Apple Health

Gymtwin asks for permission to read these Apple Health data types: workouts, height, weight, body fat percentage, biological sex and date of birth. It never writes to Apple Health. We use this data only to pre-fill your profile, your body measurements and the time of a workout you log.

Height, weight, body fat percentage, sex and date of birth read from Apple Health stay on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to our servers, never stored in iCloud, never sent to RevenueCat or Crashlytics, and never used for advertising, marketing or analytics. On a new phone, Gymtwin reads them from Health again.

When you save a workout whose time was pre-filled from an Apple Health workout, the saved Gymtwin workout (its start time and duration, the Health workout's identifier, and what you entered) is stored with your progress like any other workout, so your streak and XP can be restored. Nothing else from Apple Health is stored on our servers.

We never sell data from Apple Health or share it with third parties for advertising or any other purpose. The only copy outside your phone is the saved workout described above, stored by our service provider Google Firebase on our behalf. You can change Gymtwin's access at any time in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Gymtwin.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The Apple Health values described above.
  • The app's local copy of your progress, an unfinished live workout, and settings such as reminder times and units.
  • Streak reminders. They are scheduled on your phone as local notifications; we don't run a push notification service.

How we use information

  • To run the app: your streak, XP, levels and plan.
  • To back up your progress and restore it on another iPhone.
  • To check your subscription and unlock the app.
  • To find and fix crashes.
  • To protect our servers from abuse. Firebase App Check uses Apple's App Attest to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of Gymtwin.
  • To answer you when you contact support.

Legal bases (where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies): providing the app and your subscription (contract), keeping it stable and secure (our legitimate interests), and reading Apple Health data (your consent, which you give and can withdraw in iOS).

What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising software in the app.
  • No tracking. We don't track you across apps or websites owned by other companies and we don't access the advertising identifier (IDFA), so Gymtwin never asks for permission to track you.
  • No analytics software. If that ever changes, we'll update this policy first.
  • No selling or renting of your data, and no sharing it for advertising.

Service providers

These companies process data for us, under their own terms, to run Gymtwin. They may store it in the United States and other countries.

ProviderWhat they do for Gymtwin
Google Firebase (Google LLC) Anonymous sign-in and Apple account linking, storing your progress, deleting your account, request verification (App Check) and crash reports (Crashlytics).
RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) Checking whether your subscription is active.
Apple Sign in with Apple, App Store payments, App Attest, and Apple Health on your device.

How long we keep data

  • Account and progress: as long as your account exists, until you use Delete my data.
  • Crash reports: Crashlytics keeps them for 90 days.
  • Purchase records: RevenueCat and Apple keep transaction records as long as they need them to manage subscriptions and meet legal, tax and accounting duties. Email us if you want your RevenueCat record deleted.
  • Support emails: as long as we need them to help you.

Deleting the app removes the data on your phone but not the copy on our servers, so use Delete my data first. If you backed up with Apple, you can also reinstall, choose "I have a backup", and delete from there. Without a backup, the server copy is tied only to a random ID that we can't link back to you.

Deleting your data

In Gymtwin, open Settings (or the subscription screen) and tap Delete my data, then confirm. This permanently deletes your account and all of your progress records and body values from our servers, revokes the Sign in with Apple link if you set one up, and erases Gymtwin's data on your phone. It can't be undone.

Deleting your data doesn't cancel your subscription; manage that in your Apple ID settings. Data in Apple Health stays in Apple Health. If you can't use the app, email us and we'll help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict how it's used, and to withdraw consent. Email us to use these rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We don't sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

Children

Gymtwin is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used Gymtwin, contact us and we'll help delete the data.

Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and our providers. Each account can only read and write its own records, and our servers check that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there's less to protect.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or ads. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers. The contact form doesn't send anything by itself; it opens your own email app with your message. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change affects how Apple Health data is handled, we'll update this page before the app changes.
