Privacy Policy

What Gym Twins collects, what stays on your iPhone, and how to delete it.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

The short version

  • Height, weight, body fat, sex and date of birth read from Apple Health stay on your iPhone. They go to the Spot AI coach only if you turn on a separate switch, "Share Apple Health values".
  • Your workouts, check-ins, plan, gems and any body values you type in are backed up under a random account ID. We don't ask for your name or email address.
  • Spot, Food and AI programs send data to OpenAI only after you allow it, only when you ask, and only for adults on a free trial or subscription. Your chats and meals are kept on your phone.
  • Hardmode gym photos never leave your phone. A meal photo, or the photos from a gym scan, are sent once for analysis and are never stored.
  • Your gyms, their equipment, cover photos, places and programs are kept on your phone. Location is used only while you use the app, it is read on your phone, and it is never sent to us.
  • Weekly leagues are optional and for ages 18 and up. Other players in your league see your twin's name, look, level, medals, achievements, League XP and rank, never your workouts, weight or body data. League notifications use Apple's push service.
  • Gym Twins counts which screens people reach with Google Analytics for Firebase. It is anonymous: no name, no health values, no age and no advertising ID. It is on by default, and you can turn it off in Settings, under Usage data, once you are in the app.
  • No ads, no tracking across apps and websites, no advertising identifier (IDFA), no attribution software, and we never sell your data.
  • Delete my data in Settings erases your account and progress from our servers and everything Gym Twins keeps on your phone.
  • Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar US state laws ask for a separate policy about health and fitness data. Ours is the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Who we are

Gym Twins is developed and published by Asım Can Yağız ("we", "us"), who decides how personal data in the app is used and is the data controller. You can reach us at apputopiacy@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account identifiers

  • Anonymous account. When you first open Gym Twins, the app creates an anonymous account with Google Firebase Authentication. It is identified by a random user ID. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Back up with Apple (optional). If you choose it, we link your Sign in with Apple identity to the same account, so you can restore your progress on another iPhone and use friends. Apple gives us a unique identifier for your Apple Account. Gym Twins asks Apple for no name and no email address.
  • Install ID. The app creates a random identifier when it's installed and attaches it to each record you save, so we can tell which of your devices wrote it. It is not the advertising identifier (IDFA) and it is never used for tracking. Software inside the app creates random identifiers of its own too: Google Analytics' app-instance ID (see Anonymous usage statistics below), Firebase's installation ID and RevenueCat's own random anonymous ID, which it uses until the app tells it your account ID. Turning off "Share anonymous usage data" or using Delete my data resets the Google Analytics one on your phone.

Your workouts and progress

To keep your streak, XP and level safe, the app saves a log of what you do. Your level, streak and XP are worked out from this log each time, which is why a restore gives the same result. The log is kept on your phone and backed up on our servers under your account, whether or not you back up with Apple:

  • Workouts you save: start time, duration, the body regions you trained, how hard it felt, whether the time came from a live workout, Apple Health or manual entry, and any exercises with sets, weight and reps. If a workout was pre-filled from an Apple Health workout, we also store that Health workout's identifier so the same workout never counts twice.
  • Rest-day check-ins, and a note when you remove a workout.
  • Your training plan (the weekdays you train), your goal and your training experience, plus the optional answers you give in onboarding: a goal weight, how active you are day to day, where you train, how long a session usually lasts, the body areas you want to focus on, and what motivates you or gets in your way.
  • Your twin's look and name (the name can be up to 60 characters) and your starting level, plus progress bonuses the app grants.
  • Streak freezes and level shields you use, and the gem, quest, shop and boost records described below.
  • When your subscription became active or inactive, so the app can pause your streak while you're not subscribed.
  • Hardmode records, described below.
  • Your time zone and the day each record belongs to, plus when your account was created and last synced.

Without Back up with Apple, these records are tied to the anonymous account on this phone and can't be moved to a new phone.

Body values you enter yourself

If you type in your height, weight, body fat percentage (or accept a body-fat range or estimate in onboarding), sex or birth year in Gym Twins instead of reading them from Apple Health, those values are stored with your progress. We use them for your starting level, bonuses and calorie target. We use your birth year, together with the age range Apple shares where your iPhone supports it (see Children and age), to check whether you are 18 or older, which decides whether Spot, Food and AI programs are available and whether Gym Twins works out a calorie target for you.

Gems, quests, shop, medals and achievements

You earn gems from workouts, daily quests, medals and achievements, and spend them in the Shop on streak freezes, level shields and XP boosts. Gems are never sold for money. Each gem grant, shop purchase and boost activation is a record in your progress log (the source and amount of a grant, the item and price of a purchase), so a restore brings back the same balance. These records hold game values only and no health data.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions are sold by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription or free trial is active. RevenueCat receives your Gym Twins user ID, Apple's identifier for vendors (IDFV, a random ID Apple gives to all apps from the same developer on your device; it is not the advertising identifier), your App Store purchase information (such as the product, purchase and renewal dates, and transaction identifiers) and technical data its software sends with each request (such as IP address, App Store country, app version and device model). We don't send RevenueCat your name, email address or any health or fitness data. We never see your payment card details.

When you use Spot or Hardmode, our server also asks RevenueCat whether your account has an active trial or subscription and keeps a short-lived copy of the answer (active or not, trial or paid, expiry time) with your AI settings record.

Anonymous usage statistics

Gym Twins uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service from Google, to count how people move through the app. We use the counts to see where people drop out of onboarding and the paywall and to improve Gym Twins. It is on by default, and you can turn it off in Settings once you are in the app.

  • What the app sends. A fixed list of 24 events that we chose. They say which onboarding, paywall and offer screens you reached or skipped; whether you tapped the button that fills your details from Apple Health (the tap only, no values); how you made your commitment (hold or accessibility); whether you allowed reminders; which plan was selected, started, cancelled, failed or bought, and whether a trial started; the result of a restore; whether you chose trial reminders or to back up your progress; and whether your twin was created and a first workout or rest day was saved. Each event carries only short fixed values: a screen or step ID, a plan name, where a purchase started (the paywall or the offer), a result (allowed, denied, restored, nothing to restore, failed, linked or skipped), how you made your commitment (hold or accessibility), or a yes or no value.
  • What Google's software adds by itself. Its standard events, such as first open, session start, engagement time, app and iOS updates and a crash event, each with a random app-instance ID, the app version, iOS version, device model, language, session and engagement times, and an approximate country and city that Google works out from your IP address (Google masks the address).
  • What it never gets. The answers you give: your name, age or birth year, goals, body values, workouts, Apple Health data, account ID, advertising identifier or any user property. Which screens you reach can hint at an answer, for example whether a goal-weight screen was shown. The Meet Spot screen, which only adults see, sends no usage event. Gym Twins sets no user ID and no user property in Google Analytics, and it doesn't use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or Apple's identifier for vendors there. The data is not linked to your account.
  • Why. Only to understand where people drop out and to improve Gym Twins. Not for ads: Google Signals and ad personalization are off, and nothing in it is used to track you.
  • Who receives it. Google LLC, as our service provider. We keep Google's optional data-sharing settings off, so Google doesn't use this data for its own products and services.
  • On your phone. A few of the events are sent only once. The app remembers on your phone that it already sent them.
  • How long. Google Analytics keeps event-level data for up to 14 months and then deletes it automatically. Counts without identifiers stay in its standard reports.
  • Turn it off. In Settings, under Usage data, switch off "Share anonymous usage data". This stops sharing at once and resets the Google Analytics app-instance ID and anything waiting to be sent on your phone. It can't pull back what Google already received: that data isn't linked to your account, so we can't find yours, and it expires as described below. The switch doesn't affect crash reports. Delete my data also resets the ID and puts the switch back to its default, which is on.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report with technical details about the crash and your device, such as the device model, iOS version, app version and an identifier for the app installation that Firebase creates. Crashlytics and Firebase Sessions also send details about each app session, such as the app's bundle ID, iOS version, SDK version, network type and how long the app was in the background. We don't attach your user ID, your health or fitness data, or custom logs to crash reports. When anonymous usage statistics are on, a crash report may also include the last few usage events (event names and their values: screen and step IDs, plan names, where a purchase started (the paywall or the offer), how you made your commitment (hold or accessibility), results (allowed, denied, restored, nothing to restore, failed, linked or skipped), and yes or no values). The standard events that Google's software adds by itself, listed above, can appear in the same list. Crash reports are not part of the usage statistics switch, and the app has no separate switch for them. Crash reporting is switched off in development builds, and the app stops sending crash reports while the age block described under Children and age lasts. When a block starts, the app also deletes any crash reports that were still waiting on your phone to be sent.

Widgets, Live Activities and reminders

  • Widgets and Live Activities show information stored on your device. The app keeps a small snapshot (your twin's name and level, your streak, whether today is kept, and how much XP you earned each day) in a file shared between the app and its widgets on your phone. It is excluded from backups and is never uploaded. It holds no body measurements.
  • On the Lock Screen, a workout Live Activity can show your twin's name, level and streak, timers, the current exercise name and the set's weight and reps. Anyone who can see your Lock Screen can see it. The "Always Show" option is off unless you switch it on, and you can switch Live Activities off in iOS Settings.
  • Apple Watch. If you use the Gym Twins watch app, your iPhone sends it your twin's name and level, your streak and, during a workout, the exercise, weight and reps. It travels over Apple's iPhone-to-watch connection only, nothing is sent to our servers, and Delete my data or an age block clears it.
  • Reminders (streak reminders, the rest timer, the backup reminder, Hardmode warnings and shared-streak reminders) are scheduled on your phone as local notifications. League notifications are the only push notifications; see Weekly leagues below.

Sharing cards

Share cards (your twin, streak, workout or evolution) are drawn in memory on your phone and handed to the iOS share sheet. They go only to the app or person you choose, and Gym Twins doesn't save them to your photo library or upload them. A card can show your twin's name, level, streak, XP and medal names. It never shows weight, body fat, height, calories, workout duration or time.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. "Contact support" and "Report a problem" in the app open a draft in your own email app, which you review and send. The "Report a problem" draft quotes the Spot reply you're reporting and the app version. It contains no account ID and no health data.

Apple Health

Gym Twins asks for permission to read these Apple Health data types: workouts, height, weight, body fat percentage, biological sex and date of birth. It never writes to Apple Health. We use this data to pre-fill your profile, your body measurements and the time of a workout you log.

Height, weight, body fat percentage, sex and date of birth read from Apple Health stay on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to our servers, never stored in iCloud, never sent to RevenueCat or Crashlytics, and never used for advertising, marketing or analytics. On a new phone, Gym Twins reads them from Health again.

There are two things to know:

  • When you save a workout whose time was pre-filled from an Apple Health workout, the saved Gym Twins workout (its start time and duration, the Health workout's identifier, and what you entered) is stored with your progress like any other workout, so your streak and XP can be restored. Nothing else from Apple Health is stored on our servers.
  • If you join a weekly league, that saved workout time can count toward your League XP, and other players see only the League XP total. No Health value is shown to anyone.
  • If you turn on Share Apple Health values in Settings, then under AI & privacy, Spot may send your Health height, weight, body fat, sex and age (worked out from your date of birth; the date itself isn't sent) to OpenAI when it needs them. This is a separate choice from using Spot, it is off by default, and you can turn it off at any time. See the Spot section below.

We never sell data from Apple Health or share it with third parties for advertising or any other purpose. You can change Gym Twins' access at any time in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Gym Twins.

Spot, Food and programs (AI)

Spot is Gym Twins' AI coach. It powers the Coach tab (chat and workout suggestions), Food (a calorie and macro estimate from a meal photo), AI-written workout programs and gym scans (finding the equipment in photos or a video of your gym). These features are powered by a model from OpenAI, which acts as our processor.

When Spot can run

  • Only with your consent. Before Spot's first use, a screen lists what is sent and asks you to tap Allow. Nothing is sent before that. You can turn Spot off at any time in Settings, then AI & privacy, and the server refuses requests once consent is withdrawn.
  • Only when you ask. Spot never runs in the background. A request is sent when you send a chat message, ask for a suggestion or program, tap "Estimate calories" on a meal photo, or scan a gym.
  • Only for adults. Spot, Food, Spot's programs and gym scans are for ages 18 and up, based on the birth year in your profile and, on iPhones that support it, the age range Apple shares. If either one says you are under 18, or you have not given a birth year, these features stay off and Gym Twins' rule-based features still work.
  • Only on a free trial or active subscription. Our server checks this with RevenueCat before each request.

What is sent to OpenAI

Each request goes from the app to our server, which forwards it to OpenAI. The app never talks to OpenAI directly and holds no OpenAI key. Depending on the request, OpenAI receives:

  • Your message and Spot's earlier replies in the chat (a limited number of recent turns).
  • A training summary: your recent workouts (date, minutes, body regions, effort and whether you logged exercises), your best lifts (exercise name, weight, reps and date), your goal, experience level and weekly plan, today's date, your level and streak, and which body regions you've neglected.
  • Body details you typed in yourself (height, weight, body fat, sex and age, not your birth year) and your daily calorie totals, for chat only. Program and suggestion requests leave these out.
  • Your choices for programs and suggestions (where you train, session length, weekdays, focus, goal and level), your units (metric or imperial), your time zone, and a yes or no flag that you're 18 or older. When Spot builds a program for one of your gyms, the request includes that gym's equipment list, not its name, address, place or photos.
  • A meal photo and the optional note you type, when you scan a meal. The photo is taken with Gym Twins' own camera (a meal photo can't be picked from your library), reduced to at most 1,024 pixels on its long side, stripped of metadata such as location, and held in memory. Gym Twins does not save it on your phone or on our server.
  • The photos from a gym scan, when you scan a gym: up to 12 photos, or up to 12 frames taken on your phone from up to three videos. You take them with Gym Twins' camera or pick them with Apple's photo picker, which hands Gym Twins only the photos or videos you choose. Videos are recorded without sound. Each picture is reduced to at most 1,024 pixels on its long side and stripped of metadata such as location. They are sent once, to find the gym equipment in them, and Gym Twins keeps them only in memory and a temporary folder until the scan is done, then deletes them. You can scan 3 times a day, or once a day on a free trial.
  • Apple Health height, weight, body fat, sex and age, only if you turned on "Share Apple Health values".

Never sent to OpenAI: your name, email address, Apple ID, location (including your gyms' addresses and places), your gyms' names and cover photos, your twin's name, your account ID, or anything from Apple Health unless you turned on the separate Health switch. Gems, the shop, achievements, Hardmode, shared streaks, widget data and share cards are never part of a request.

What our server does and does not keep

  • It checks your account and that the request comes from a genuine copy of the app, checks your subscription and consent, counts the request against your daily and monthly limits, and passes it on. These limits control cost and abuse.
  • It does not store or log your messages, photos, training summary or Spot's replies.
  • It keeps a record of your AI consent (which version you agreed to, when, and whether Health values are included), your time zone (used only to decide when your daily limit resets), the short-lived subscription check, and usage counters (how many requests of each kind per day and per month, and their approximate cost).
  • Its operational logs hold the request type, model, whether it succeeded, timing, token counts, cost and a hashed account ID. They hold no content.
  • It screens your chat message for safety-related phrases (for example about self-harm or eating disorders). A match changes how Spot answers and shows a card with helplines. Nothing is stored because of it.

What OpenAI does with it

  • OpenAI uses the request only to produce Spot's reply. We ask it not to keep the conversation as application state.
  • OpenAI may keep API requests, including a meal photo or gym scan photos, for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse. It does not use data sent through its API to train its models.
  • Each request carries a hashed version of your account ID (a keyed one-way hash, not your real ID) that OpenAI uses for abuse monitoring.

What stays on your phone

Your Spot chat and your food log (meals with name, calories and macros) are stored only on your phone, in a private folder that is excluded from backups and not shared with widgets. A new phone starts empty. Programs Spot writes are kept with your gyms, as described in Gyms and location. In Settings, then AI & privacy, you can clear your chat, delete your food log or Spot's programs, or delete all AI data on the device. Delete my data removes them too.

Limits of AI

Spot's answers and calorie estimates can be wrong. They are not medical advice. See the Terms of Use.

Gyms and location

You can save the gyms you train at, list the equipment each one has, and keep programs for each gym. Programs come from ready-made templates or from Spot.

What stays on your phone

  • Your gyms (name, equipment list, cover photo, address and place) and their programs (yours, from templates or from Spot) are stored only on your iPhone, in a folder shared between the app and its widgets. They are part of your iPhone's iCloud backup if you use one, so they come back when you restore that backup. Gym Twins never uploads them, and they are not synced through iCloud by Gym Twins.
  • Cover photos are taken with Gym Twins' camera or picked with Apple's photo picker, which hands Gym Twins only the photo you choose. Gym Twins can't see the rest of your photo library and doesn't ask for access to it.
  • Delete my data erases your gyms, their cover photos and every program. In Settings, then AI & privacy, deleting programs removes only the ones Spot wrote.

Gym scans

Instead of picking equipment from a list, you can scan a gym with Spot. This needs Spot's consent, is for ages 18 and up, and sends the photos once to OpenAI through our server, which doesn't store them. See What is sent to OpenAI for the details and limits.

Location

  • Only while you use the app. Gym Twins asks for location "While Using the App", and only when you tap "Use my current location" while adding a gym. You can say no and still add a gym by searching for it, or skip its place.
  • Read on your phone. If at least one of your gyms has a place and you allowed location, Gym Twins reads your position once when the Workouts tab opens, to suggest the program for the gym you're at. The position stays in memory and is never saved.
  • Never sent. Your position and your gyms' addresses and places are never sent to us, to OpenAI or to analytics. Gym Twins uses no background location and no geofences.
  • Apple Maps. When you search for a gym or an address, the text you type goes to Apple Maps (MapKit) to find results, and the map preview loads map images from Apple. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.

Friends, Hardmode and Gym Dash

Friends and shared streaks

You can share a streak with a friend, one friend per streak and up to three streaks at once. To invite someone or accept an invite, you first need Back up with Apple. There is no chat and no friend search.

  • Before you invite, the app shows what your friend will see: your twin's name, its level and whether you kept the day. Nothing is created until you tap "Create invite link".
  • What we store for an invite: a one-time 8-character code that works for 7 days, and a card with your twin's name (cut to 20 characters), look, level and last kept day. The invite link opens a small page on Firebase Hosting that shows the code and a button to open the app. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
  • When a streak is shared, each of you can see the other's twin name, look, level and last kept day, plus the shared streak counters (current count, best count and the results of recent days). Your friend can't see your workouts, exercises, weights, body values, photos or gems.
  • To decide whether you both kept a day, our server reads each member's progress records. The result is a verdict for the shared streak, not a copy of your records.
  • Twin card in invites. By default, an invite message includes a picture of your twin card, and you can switch that off. The picture goes only to the app you choose and is never uploaded to us.
  • Safety. Twin names are checked against a word list and by pattern checks for web links, email addresses, @ handles and phone numbers. You can report your friend's twin name or behavior (a report stores the reason, who reported whom, the streak, the twin name at the time and the date), block them, or leave the streak. Reports are only visible to us.
  • We also keep rate-limit counters (such as how many invites or reports you made today) and your block list, in a record only the server can read.

Hardmode

Hardmode is optional. When it is on, each workout needs a gym photo taken with Gym Twins' own camera (you can't pick one from your library).

  • The photo never leaves your phone. It is saved without metadata (such as location) in the app's private folder, excluded from backups. It is removed after 90 days, or sooner if you delete it from your Profile or use Delete my data. We don't upload it, analyze it or show it to anyone, including friends.
  • What our servers get are records that Hardmode started or ended and that a photo was taken for a workout (the workout ID and the time), as part of your progress log. While you are in Hardmode, our server reads your log about once an hour to decide whether you kept each Hardmode day, and writes the verdict (for example "died" or "cleared") for you. Before it judges, it checks with RevenueCat that your subscription is active.
  • This verdict is an automated decision about a game. It has no legal or similarly significant effect on you.

Gym Dash

Gym Dash, the game you can play during a rest timer, runs entirely on your phone. It saves only your best distance, in the app's preferences on the device. It sends nothing and reads none of your progress.

Weekly leagues

Weekly leagues are optional and for people aged 18 and over. Each week you are placed in a group with other players and the Gym Twins crew, ranked by League XP, and moved up or down a league when the week ends. The top three get gems.

What other players see

  • Your card: your twin's name (cut to 20 characters and checked against a word list), its look and level, which medals (7, 30, 100 and 365 days) and achievements you have earned, your League XP and your rank.
  • Never: your workouts, exercises, weights, body values, Apple Health values, photos, gems, current streak or account details. Use a nickname, not your real name.
  • How long: a board is kept for 8 weeks. If you leave leagues or delete your data, your rows on stored boards become "Gym Twins user": your name, look, medals and achievements are removed, and only the score and rank stay so the board stays whole.
  • On your phone: the app keeps a copy of your current board, including the other players' cards, so it can show it offline. It is deleted when the week's board is closed, when you leave leagues and when you use Delete my data.

The Gym Twins crew

Every league group includes characters called the Gym Twins crew (animals and costumed characters). They are part of the game, not real people. Their scores are set by the game. Each group's target comes from the median League XP of real players across all leagues in earlier weeks, and never depends on the workouts or scores of the players in your group.

How League XP is worked out

  • Our server works out League XP from your workout log: the minutes and effort of your logged workouts, with the same limits for everyone (90 counted minutes a day and 630 a week). It leaves out boosts, the Hardmode bonus and the body-fat bonus. A workout whose time came from Apple Health can count.
  • Other players see only the total, never the workouts behind it.
  • When a week ends, our server checks with RevenueCat that your subscription or free trial is active. If it isn't, your result for that week is frozen: no promotion, no demotion and no gems. A week under 100 League XP (about 40 minutes of training) is frozen the same way, so a short week never moves you down.
  • Our server looks for signs of cheating, for example a week at the daily limit every day or workout time that is mostly typed in. It keeps a record of those signals for 90 days. After a review, we may place you only in separate groups from the next week, without telling you. This never changes a result that was already given.

League notifications

  • They are off until you turn them on. Once they are on, you can get one when someone passes you, in the last hours of the week and when the week's result is in. You choose which ones in Settings, and you can switch them off in iOS Settings.
  • They are sent through Apple's push notification service. To send them, the app gives our server the token Apple creates for your phone, and we store it with your account ID. We don't use Firebase Cloud Messaging. The token is deleted when you leave leagues, when you delete your data, when Apple says it is no longer valid, or after 60 days without a refresh.
  • A notification can show the name and picture of the player who passed you. iOS may keep that name in Siri suggestions and the share sheet on your phone; Gym Twins deletes those entries each time it opens.

Reporting, blocking and leaving

In Gym Twins, open Settings (or the subscription screen, or the screen that says Gym Twins is for ages 13 and over) and tap Delete my data, then confirm. This:

Children and age

Gym Twins is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly keep personal data from children under 13: the app asks for a birth year and blocks anyone whose answer makes them under 13, as described below. If you believe a child under 13 has used Gym Twins, contact us and we'll help delete the data.

Spot, Food and AI programs are for ages 18 and up. The app checks the birth year in your profile and, on iPhones that support it, the age range Apple shares (see Apple's age range below). If either one says you are under 18, or you haven't given a birth year, these features are off, no data is sent to OpenAI, and Gym Twins doesn't work out a calorie target or body-fat bonus for you. Your birth year is something you tell the app and the app can't verify it.

The app counts a birth year as the youngest age that person could be: someone who says they were born in 2013 counts as born on 31 December 2013. The birth year question offers every year, including years that make you under 13, so a child who gives the true answer is blocked. If a birth year stored in Gym Twins, or read from Apple Health, makes you under 13, or Apple's age range says you are under 13, Gym Twins shows a screen that blocks the app. When the block starts, the app:

  • deletes the copy of your data on our servers (your backup) and your account there, without asking you to sign in with Apple;
  • stops sending crash reports and usage statistics, and deletes crash reports that were still waiting on your phone to be sent;
  • keeps the data on your phone until you use Delete my data, which is on every block screen, including the one you can reach while setting up the app.

While the block lasts, the app also stops syncing to our servers, planning reminders, publishing your friends card, showing Live Activities, updating the widget snapshot and sending anything to your Apple Watch.

The deletion on our servers is automatic. It shows no Sign in with Apple screen and asks a child to do nothing. Unlike Delete my data, which you start yourself, it does not revoke the Sign in with Apple link on Apple's side. If you had set that link up, you can remove it in your Apple Account settings. If our servers can't be reached, the app tries again the next time it opens. Until the deletion succeeds, the app keeps a yes-or-no marker on your phone saying that a deletion is still owed. It holds no year or age, it is never sent anywhere, and the app removes it once the deletion succeeds or the block lifts.

If you give a birth year under 13 while setting up the app, whether you pick it or Apple Health holds it, the app doesn't store the year, but it remembers on your phone that an under-13 answer was given, so closing and reopening the app doesn't let anyone answer again. That memory is a single yes-or-no marker. It holds no year, no age and no date, and it is never sent anywhere. It stays until you use Delete my data, or until the app finds a stored birth year that makes you 13 or over.

A missing birth year doesn't block the app. Spot, Food, AI programs, gym scans, calorie targets and the monthly body-fat bonus follow the same rule: they need the youngest possible age to be 18 or more, so they stay off when the birth year is missing. Anonymous usage statistics carry no age or other personal value, so they are on by default for every age the app allows (13 and over), and anyone can switch them off.

Weekly leagues are for ages 18 and up and follow the same rule, including Apple's age range described below. See Weekly leagues above.

Apple's age range

On iPhones with iOS 26.2 or later, Gym Twins can ask Apple for your age range, using Apple's Declared Age Range feature, with the cut-offs 13 and 18. Depending on where you are, Apple shares the range itself or asks you first, and you may be able to say no. Apple gives the app a range only (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over), never your birth date. Gym Twins keeps the range in memory while the app runs. It never saves the range itself, never sends it to our servers or to anyone else, and never puts it in usage statistics or crash reports. If the range causes a block, the app keeps the same block markers on your phone that it keeps for any block (yes-or-no notes that a block started and that a deletion on our servers is still owed, with no range, year or age in them). Those markers never leave your phone. It uses the range only to apply the age rules on this page:

  • If Apple says you are under 13, the block above applies.
  • If Apple says you are 13 to 17, Spot, Food, AI programs, gym scans, weekly leagues, calorie targets and the body-fat bonus stay off, even if your birth year says 18 or more.
  • If Apple would share your age range and you decline, or the request fails or times out, Gym Twins treats you as 13 to 17 for those features until the range comes through. A "Share age range" option in the app opens your iPhone's settings for that.
  • If your iPhone doesn't support the feature, or Apple doesn't ask for your age range where you are, only the birth year you gave is used.

The stricter of the two always applies. Apple's answer never loosens a rule, and Gym Twins never changes the birth year you entered.
