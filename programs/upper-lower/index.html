Upper Lower workout plan

Upper Lower is an intermediate plan with 4 workout days of about 45 minutes each. Day 1, upper a: Barbell Bench Press, Barbell Row, Dumbbell Overhead Press, Wide Grip Lat Pulldown, Dumbbell Curl, and Cable Triceps Pushdown. Day 2, lower a: Barbell Back Squat, Lying Leg Curl Machine, Leg Press, Standing Calf Raise, and Cable Crunch. Day 3, upper b: Incline Dumbbell Press, Seated Cable Row, Dumbbell Lateral Raise, Cable Face Pull, Dumbbell Hammer Curl, and Barbell Skull Crusher. Day 4, lower b: Barbell Deadlift, Dumbbell Lunge, Barbell Hip Thrust, Leg Extension, Standing Calf Raise, and Plank.

Day 1: Upper A

Chest, Back, Shoulders, Arms

  1. Barbell Bench Press 4 sets × 6-8 reps · rest 2:00 Swap: Dumbbell Bench Press, Push Up
  2. Barbell Row 4 sets × 6-8 reps · rest 2:00 Swap: Dumbbell Bent Over Row, Dumbbell Row
  3. Dumbbell Overhead Press 3 sets × 8-10 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Pike Push Up
  4. Wide Grip Lat Pulldown 3 sets × 8-12 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Dumbbell Pullover, Dumbbell Row
  5. Dumbbell Curl 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Self Resisted Biceps Curl
  6. Cable Triceps Pushdown 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Dumbbell Triceps Extension, Bodyweight Triceps Extension

Day 2: Lower A

Legs, Core

  1. Barbell Back Squat 4 sets × 6-8 reps · rest 2:30 Swap: Dumbbell Squat, Bodyweight Squat
  2. Lying Leg Curl Machine 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:15 Swap: Dumbbell Deadlift, Glute Bridge
  3. Leg Press 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Dumbbell Lunge, Reverse Lunge
  4. Standing Calf Raise 4 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 45 s
  5. Cable Crunch 3 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 45 s Swap: Crunch

Day 3: Upper B

Chest, Back, Shoulders, Arms

  1. Incline Dumbbell Press 4 sets × 8-10 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Dumbbell Floor Press, Push Up
  2. Seated Cable Row 4 sets × 8-10 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Dumbbell Row, Bird Dog Row
  3. Dumbbell Lateral Raise 3 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Prone T Raise
  4. Cable Face Pull 3 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Dumbbell Rear Delt Raise, Prone T Raise
  5. Dumbbell Hammer Curl 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Self Resisted Biceps Curl
  6. Barbell Skull Crusher 3 sets × 8-12 reps · rest 1:15 Swap: Dumbbell Triceps Extension, Bodyweight Triceps Extension

Day 4: Lower B

Legs, Core

  1. Barbell Deadlift 3 sets × 5-6 reps · rest 3:00 Swap: Dumbbell Deadlift, Glute Bridge
  2. Dumbbell Lunge 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:15 Swap: Reverse Lunge
  3. Barbell Hip Thrust 3 sets × 8-12 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Dumbbell Hip Thrust, Glute Bridge
  4. Leg Extension 3 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Bodyweight Squat
  5. Standing Calf Raise 4 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 45 s
  6. Plank 3 sets × 20-30 reps · rest 45 s Swap: Dead Bug

Equipment

You need barbell and plates, flat bench, dumbbells, lat pulldown, cable crossover, squat rack, leg curl, leg press, adjustable bench, seated cable row, and leg extension. Each exercise lists a swap if your gym doesn't have the main one.
