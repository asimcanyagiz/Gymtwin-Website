Beginner · 3 days · about 45 min

Full Body Basics workout plan

Full Body Basics is a beginner plan with 3 workout days of about 45 minutes each. Day 1, Full body A: Leg Press, Dumbbell Bench Press, Seated Cable Row, Dumbbell Overhead Press, Lying Leg Curl Machine, and Plank. Day 2, Full body B: Dumbbell Squat, Incline Dumbbell Press, Wide Grip Lat Pulldown, Dumbbell Lateral Raise, Glute Bridge, and Dead Bug. Day 3, Full body C: Dumbbell Deadlift, Chest Press Machine, Dumbbell Row, Cable Face Pull, Dumbbell Curl, and Crunch.

Day 1: Full body A

Full Body

  1. Leg Press 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Dumbbell Squat, Bodyweight Squat
  2. Dumbbell Bench Press 3 sets × 8-12 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Push Up
  3. Seated Cable Row 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:15 Swap: Dumbbell Row, Bird Dog Row
  4. Dumbbell Overhead Press 3 sets × 8-12 reps · rest 1:15 Swap: Pike Push Up
  5. Lying Leg Curl Machine 2 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Dumbbell Deadlift, Glute Bridge
  6. Plank 3 sets × 20-30 reps · rest 45 s Swap: Dead Bug

Day 2: Full body B

Full Body

  1. Dumbbell Squat 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Bodyweight Squat
  2. Incline Dumbbell Press 3 sets × 8-12 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Dumbbell Floor Press, Push Up
  3. Wide Grip Lat Pulldown 3 sets × 8-12 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Dumbbell Pullover, Dumbbell Row
  4. Dumbbell Lateral Raise 2 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Prone T Raise
  5. Glute Bridge 3 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 1:00
  6. Dead Bug 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 45 s Swap: Crunch

Day 3: Full body C

Full Body

  1. Dumbbell Deadlift 3 sets × 8-10 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Glute Bridge
  2. Chest Press Machine 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Dumbbell Bench Press, Push Up
  3. Dumbbell Row 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:15 Swap: Bird Dog Row
  4. Cable Face Pull 3 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Dumbbell Rear Delt Raise, Prone T Raise
  5. Dumbbell Curl 2 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Self Resisted Biceps Curl
  6. Crunch 3 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 45 s Swap: Reverse Crunch

Equipment

You need leg press, dumbbells, flat bench, seated cable row, leg curl, adjustable bench, lat pulldown, chest press machine, and cable crossover. Each exercise lists a swap if your gym doesn't have the main one.
