Legs and Glutes workout plan

Legs and Glutes is an all-levels plan with 3 workout days of about 45 minutes each. Day 1, Legs A: Leg Press, Barbell Hip Thrust, Dumbbell Lunge, Lying Leg Curl Machine, and Standing Calf Raise. Day 2, Legs B: Barbell Back Squat, Dumbbell Deadlift, Leg Extension, Single Leg Glute Bridge, and Standing Calf Raise. Day 3, Legs C: Barbell Deadlift, Dumbbell Hip Thrust, Reverse Lunge, Leg Press, and Lying Leg Curl Machine.

Day 1: Legs A

Legs

  1. Leg Press 4 sets × 8-10 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Dumbbell Squat, Bodyweight Squat
  2. Barbell Hip Thrust 3 sets × 8-12 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Dumbbell Hip Thrust, Glute Bridge
  3. Dumbbell Lunge 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:15 Swap: Reverse Lunge
  4. Lying Leg Curl Machine 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Dumbbell Deadlift, Single Leg Glute Bridge
  5. Standing Calf Raise 3 sets × 15-20 reps · rest 45 s

Day 2: Legs B

Legs

  1. Barbell Back Squat 4 sets × 6-8 reps · rest 2:00 Swap: Dumbbell Squat, Bodyweight Squat
  2. Dumbbell Deadlift 3 sets × 8-10 reps · rest 1:30 Swap: Glute Bridge
  3. Leg Extension 3 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Reverse Lunge
  4. Single Leg Glute Bridge 3 sets × 8-12 reps · rest 45 s
  5. Standing Calf Raise 3 sets × 15-20 reps · rest 45 s

Day 3: Legs C

Legs

  1. Barbell Deadlift 3 sets × 5-6 reps · rest 2:30 Swap: Dumbbell Deadlift, Glute Bridge
  2. Dumbbell Hip Thrust 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:15 Swap: Glute Bridge
  3. Reverse Lunge 3 sets × 10-12 reps · rest 1:00
  4. Leg Press 3 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 1:15 Swap: Dumbbell Squat, Bodyweight Squat
  5. Lying Leg Curl Machine 3 sets × 12-15 reps · rest 1:00 Swap: Single Leg Glute Bridge

Equipment

You need leg press, barbell and plates, flat bench, dumbbells, leg curl, squat rack, and leg extension. Each exercise lists a swap if your gym doesn't have the main one.
