Consumer Health Data Policy

The health and fitness data Gym Twin collects, why, who gets it, and how to use your rights.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version

  • Gym Twin collects health and fitness data only to run the app: your workouts and rest days, the body values and goals you give it, and, if you use Spot, what you tell it and a meal photo.
  • Apple Health values (height, weight, body fat, sex, date of birth and workouts) stay on your iPhone. They go to OpenAI, for Spot only, if you turn on a separate switch, "Share Apple Health values".
  • We share this data with Google Firebase, which stores your progress for us; with OpenAI, only when you use Spot and have allowed it; and with a friend you invite to a shared streak, who sees only your twin's name, look, level and last kept day.
  • We never sell this data. We don't use it for ads and we don't give it to data brokers.
  • You can ask what we hold, get a copy, withdraw your consent or have it deleted. "Delete my data" in Settings is the quickest way to delete.

Who this policy is for

This is the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for the Gym Twin app for iPhone, its widgets and Live Activity, and its Apple Watch app. Gym Twin is developed and published by Asım Can Yağız ("we", "us"), who decides why and how the data in the app is collected and used.

Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) requires a separate policy like this one, linked from our home page. Nevada (SB 370) and Connecticut (in the Connecticut Data Privacy Act) have similar rules for consumer health data. We publish this policy for everyone, wherever you live. Read it together with our Privacy Policy, which covers all personal data in the app.

These laws define consumer health data as personal information that is linked or reasonably linkable to you and that identifies your past, present or future physical or mental health status. In Gym Twin that is, in our judgment, the health and fitness information listed below. We handle all of it with the care these laws ask for.

What we collect and why

This table lists each kind of consumer health data Gym Twin collects, why, and where it is kept. "Our servers" means our Firebase backend (Authentication, Firestore and Cloud Functions), where your backup is kept under a random account ID. We don't ask for your name or email address.

Category What it includes Why we collect it Where it is kept
Workouts and rest days Workouts you save (start time, duration, the body regions you trained, how hard it felt, and any exercises with sets, weight and reps), rest-day check-ins, your training plan, and the streak, XP and level worked out from them. If a workout was pre-filled from Apple Health, also that Health workout's identifier. To keep your streak and progress, and to restore them on a new phone if you back up with Apple. On your iPhone, and on our servers.
Body values you type in Height, weight, body fat percentage (or the body-fat range or estimate you accept in onboarding), sex and birth year. To set your starting level, work out bonuses and, for adults, a calorie target, and so Spot can tailor its advice if you use it. On your iPhone, and on our servers.
Goals and onboarding answers Your goal (for example building muscle or losing fat), training experience, and the optional answers you give: a goal weight, how active you are day to day, where you train, how long a session usually lasts, the body areas you want to focus on, and what motivates you or gets in your way. To set up your plan and personalize the app. On your iPhone, and on our servers.
Apple Health values Height, weight, body fat percentage, biological sex, date of birth and workouts, read from Apple Health only after you tap to allow it. Gym Twin never writes to Apple Health. To pre-fill your profile, your body measurements and the time of a workout you log. Only on your iPhone. Never on our servers, in iCloud or in analytics. The one exception: a workout you save that was pre-filled from Health is stored like any other workout (see the first row).
Meals and calories A meal photo and the optional note you type when you scan a meal, the calorie and macro estimate, your food log, and a calorie target worked out on your phone (adults only). To estimate a meal and show your day against a calorie target. The photo is held in memory and never saved. The food log and target stay on your iPhone only.
What you tell Spot Your chat messages with Spot, our AI coach (adults only). Gym Twin doesn't ask for medical conditions, medications or diagnoses. If you type them into a chat, they are handled like any other message. To answer you and suggest training. On your iPhone only. Our server passes each request on and doesn't store or log its content.
Hardmode gym photos A photo taken with Gym Twin's own camera for each Hardmode workout. We don't analyze it and Gym Twin has no face or body recognition. To count a workout in Hardmode, which asks for a gym photo as proof. Only on your iPhone, for 90 days or until you delete it. Never uploaded.

Gym Twin does not collect precise location (if you allow location to find your gym, your position is read on your phone and never sent to us; see the Privacy Policy), biometric data, or information about a medical diagnosis or treatment. Anonymous usage statistics and crash reports carry no body value, workout, goal or age. They can hint at an answer through the screens you reach, for example whether a goal-weight screen was shown; our Privacy Policy explains this.

Where it comes from

  • You. What you type or tap in the app: body values, goals, answers, workouts, rest-day check-ins, meal notes, photos you take with Gym Twin's camera and messages to Spot.
  • Apple Health on your iPhone. Only the values listed above, only after you allow it in Gym Twin and in iOS.
  • Your use of the app. For example the time and length of a live workout, and the streak, XP and level that Gym Twin works out from your records. Our server also works out whether you and a friend both kept a day, and whether you kept each Hardmode day.
  • Your Apple Watch. When you tap "Set done" on the watch, your iPhone records the set. The watch app holds only what your iPhone last sent it and has no connection of its own to our servers.

Apple receives nothing from us about your health or fitness: Apple Health stays on your iPhone, and Apple's role is limited to things like App Store purchases and Sign in with Apple.

What we never do

  • We never sell consumer health data. If that ever changed, the law would require your valid signed authorization first, and we would update this policy before it happened.
  • No ads, and no sharing of this data for advertising or with data brokers.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps and websites, and no use of the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No geofence around any place, including where health care is provided. Gym Twin uses location only on your phone, while you use the app, to find your gym, and never sends it to us.
  • No new category of this data, and no new reason to use or share it, without telling you first and asking for your consent.

Your consent and choices

Most of the data above is collected because you give it to the app so that the app can work for you. For the choices below, you decide, and you can change your mind at any time:

  • Apple Health. Gym Twin reads nothing from Apple Health until you tap "Use Apple Health" and allow it in iOS. You can change access any time in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Gym Twin.
  • Spot and OpenAI. Before Spot's first use, a screen lists what is sent and asks you to tap Allow. Nothing is sent before that. Turn it off any time in Settings, then AI & privacy, then Use Spot; this withdraws your consent and the server refuses new requests.
  • Apple Health values for Spot. A separate switch, "Share Apple Health values" in Settings, then AI & privacy. It is off by default.
  • Camera. Meal and Hardmode photos use Gym Twin's own camera, and only after you allow the camera in iOS. Gym scans and gym cover photos can also come from Apple's photo picker, which hands Gym Twin only what you choose.
  • Friends. Nothing is created, and nothing is shared with a friend, until you tap "Create invite link". You can leave, block or report from a shared streak.
  • Weekly leagues. Nothing is shared with other players until you join and accept the league rules. You can leave leagues in Settings, and your rows on stored boards then show as "Gym Twin user".

Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to:

  • confirm whether we are collecting, sharing or selling your consumer health data (we collect and share as described on this page, and we never sell it);
  • access it, including a list of all third parties and affiliates we have shared it with and an active email address or other online way to contact them (the companies and their links are in the table above);
  • withdraw your consent to our collecting and sharing it; and
  • have it deleted.

How to use them

  • Delete in the app. Open Settings (or the subscription screen), tap Delete my data and confirm. It permanently deletes your account and progress records on our servers, including your workouts, body values, goals and answers, and erases what Gym Twin keeps on your phone, such as your Spot chat, food log, programs and Hardmode photos. It can't be undone. Data in Apple Health stays in Apple Health. See the Privacy Policy for what else it does and for the purchase records that RevenueCat and Apple keep.
  • Withdraw consent in the app. Use the switches under "Your consent and choices" above.
  • Everything else, by email. Write to apputopiacy@gmail.com with the subject "Consumer health data request". Tell us whether you want to confirm, access, withdraw or delete. Because accounts are anonymous, we may need your help to find yours, for example by confirming details only the account holder would know. This is how we make sure a request comes from you.

How fast we answer

We aim to answer within 30 days, and always within 45 days of receiving your request. If we need more time, we can extend that once by 45 more days, and we will tell you why. We don't charge for requests, and we won't treat you differently for making one.

If we say no, you can appeal

If we refuse to act on your request, reply to our answer, or email apputopiacy@gmail.com with the subject "Appeal", and say why you disagree. Within 45 days we will tell you in writing what we did or didn't do about your appeal, and why. If we deny the appeal, you can complain to your state's attorney general. In Washington, you can file a complaint with the Washington Attorney General's Office.

How long we keep it and how we protect it

  • Your progress records, body values, goals and answers on our servers: as long as your account exists, until you use Delete my data. If an age block starts, we delete them then (see Age below).
  • Your Spot chat, food log, AI programs and Apple Health values: only on your iPhone, until you delete them in Settings, use Delete my data or delete the app.
  • Hardmode photos: on your iPhone only, for 90 days or until you delete them.
  • Requests at OpenAI: up to 30 days for abuse monitoring, then deleted.

The Privacy Policy has the full retention table. Data travels encrypted between the app and our providers, and Google Cloud encrypts stored data. Each account can only read and write its own records, our server checks that requests come from a genuine copy of the app, and Apple Health values never reach our servers.

Age

Gym Twin is meant for people aged 13 and over. Spot, Food, AI programs and calorie targets are for ages 18 and up. If the birth year you give, or the age range Apple shares with the app, says you are under 13, the app blocks itself, deletes the copy of your data on our servers (without asking you to sign in), stops sending crash reports and usage statistics, and deletes crash reports that were still waiting on your phone. Your data on the phone stays there until you use Delete my data, which is on every block screen. An under-13 answer given while setting up the app is remembered on the phone as a single yes-or-no marker, with no year or age in it, until you use Delete my data or the app finds a stored birth year that makes you 13 or over. The Privacy Policy has the details, including how the age range from Apple is used (the range itself is in memory only, never saved or sent).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. We won't collect a new category of consumer health data, or use or share it for a new reason, without telling you first and getting your consent.
