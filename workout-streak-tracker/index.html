Workout streak tracker

A workout streak tracker that doesn't punish rest days

Most streak apps want a workout every single day, so the first rest day breaks the chain. Gym Twin counts days you keep instead: on a gym day you log a workout of 10 minutes or more, and on a rest day a quick check-in keeps the streak alive. Your plan decides which day is which, so resting on schedule never costs you the streak.

How the streak works

  • Gym days: pick the weekdays you train. On those days, log a workout of 10 minutes or more.
  • Rest days: every other day, one tap on Rest check-in keeps the day. A workout counts too.
  • Late sessions: your day ends at 3:00 AM, not midnight, and a workout counts for the day it started.

What happens when you miss a day

The streak resets and your pixel twin gets tired. You then have 3 days: log a workout of 10 minutes or more and your twin recovers at the same level. If you don't, it shrinks back one level. A Level 1 twin can't shrink further.

Gems you earn from workouts buy a streak freeze or a level shield in the Shop, for the weeks life gets in the way.

Why a twin instead of a number

A streak counter is easy to ignore once it resets. In Gym Twin the streak feeds a pixel-art twin that grows through five levels, from Beginner to Beast Mode, as you earn XP from the minutes you train. Seeing it tired is a nudge that a plain number never gives you.

Keep it in view

A streak widget on your Lock Screen, a heat map of your training on the Home Screen, and a Live Activity with your rest timer while you work out. Invite a friend with a link and you can keep a shared streak together.

Questions

Does a rest day break my workout streak?

No. Days that aren't in your plan are rest days, and a rest check-in keeps them. Only a gym day without a workout of 10 minutes or more breaks the streak.

When does my day end?

At 3:00 AM in your time zone. A workout that starts at 11:30 PM still counts for that day.

Can I change my gym days?

Yes, in Settings. The new plan starts the next day.
