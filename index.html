Five levels

One twin. Five forms.

Every level brings six new moves for your twin to show off.

How the streak works

Every day counts. Rest days too.

Plan your training days once. Gym Twin tells you what today needs.

💪

Gym days

Pick the days you train. On those days, log a workout of 10 minutes or more.

🌙

Rest days

On your other days, a quick rest check-in keeps the streak alive. A workout counts too.

🕒

Your day ends at 3 AM

A late session counts for the day it started, and the day doesn't close until 3:00 AM.

Missed a day?

Your twin gets tired

The streak resets, and you have 3 days to bounce back.

→

Train within 3 days

Your twin recovers and keeps its level.

No workout in 3 days

Your twin shrinks back one level. Beginners can't shrink further.

Inside the app

Made for the daily habit

Train, check in, and grow your twin. Add a coach, friends and some stakes if you want them.

⚡

Log in about 10 seconds

Pick the body regions you trained, set the time, and say how it felt. Done.

⏱️

Live workouts

Track sets, weight and reps as you go, with a rest timer between sets. If the app closes mid-workout, pick up where you left off.

✨

Evolution scenes

When your twin levels up you get the full moment: a glow, a flash, and the new form. Tap to speed it up.

🤖

Spot, your AI coach

Chat about training, get a suggestion for today's session, and see which body regions you've been skipping. Spot uses AI from OpenAI, runs only when you ask, and only after you allow it. For ages 18 and up. Not medical advice.

🍽️

Food

Snap a meal for a calorie and macro estimate. The photo is used once and never saved. Add meals by hand too, and watch your day against a calorie target. Estimates only. For ages 18 and up.

📋

Programs

Ask Spot for a 4-week program built around the days you train, the equipment you have and the time you have. Start any program day as a live workout.

📚

Exercise library

Browse more than 90 exercises by body region and equipment. It works offline and needs no account.

🤝

Friends and shared streaks

Invite a friend with a link and keep a streak together. You see each other's twin name, look, level and whether the day was kept, and nothing else. Report, block or leave any time.

💀

Hardmode

For people who want stakes. Every workout needs a gym photo that stays on your phone, XP is 1.5x, and a missed day costs your twin a level. Miss a day at Level 1 and your twin dies. Optional, and you can leave any time.

🎮

Gym Dash

A quick runner for your rest timer: tap to jump, swipe down to duck under the pull-up bar. It plays offline and sends nothing.

💎

Gems and daily quests

Earn gems from workouts, daily quests and milestones. Gems can't be bought, so every one means you showed up.

🛡️

Freeze, shield and boost

Spend gems in the Shop on a streak freeze, a level shield or an XP boost. They don't work in Hardmode.

🏅

Medals and achievements

Medals for long streaks and achievements to collect. Each one pays out gems once.

📱

Widgets and Live Activity

A heat map on your Home Screen, a streak widget on your Lock Screen, and a workout Live Activity with your rest timer on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.

🖼️

Share cards

Share a twin, streak, workout or evolution card from the iOS share sheet. Cards never show weight, body fat or calories.

❤️

Apple Health

Pre-fill workouts and body measurements from Health. Gym Twin only reads. Health values stay on your phone and reach Spot only if you switch on a separate setting.

☁️

Back up with Apple

Optional. Sign in with Apple to keep your twin when you switch phones and to play with friends. Skip it and Gym Twin works the same; your progress just stays with this phone.

🔔

Streak reminders

A heads-up when today still needs a workout or a check-in, so a busy day doesn't cost you the streak.

🎨

Pick your twin

Choose one of five looks and give your twin a name. It's with you from the first workout.

Your Health data stays on your phone.

  • ❤️

    Gym Twin reads Apple Health and never writes to it. Height, weight, body fat, sex and birth date from Health are never uploaded. They reach Spot only if you switch on a separate setting.

  • 🤖

    Spot sends data to OpenAI only after you allow it, only when you ask, and only for adults. Hardmode photos never leave your phone, and a meal photo is used once and never saved.

  • 🚫

    No ads, no tracking across apps and websites, no analytics software, and we never sell your data.

  • 🗑️

    Delete my data in Settings removes your progress from our servers and everything Gym Twin keeps on your phone.
