Common questions

How does the streak work?

Your streak is the number of days in a row you've kept. On a gym day (a day in your plan), log a workout of 10 minutes or more. On any other day, a rest check-in keeps the day, and a workout counts too.

A check-in alone doesn't keep a gym day, which is why the app only offers it on rest days.

What counts as a rest day?

Any day that isn't in your training plan. You can change your plan in Settings; the new plan starts the next day.

When does my day end?

At 3:00 AM in your time zone, not at midnight. A workout counts for the day it started, so a session that starts at 11:30 PM and ends after midnight still counts for that day.

Quick logs are for today only. A live workout that runs past 3:00 AM still counts for the day it started.

What happens if I miss a day?

If you had a streak, it resets and your twin gets tired. Log a workout of 10 minutes or more within the next 3 days and your twin recovers at the same level.

If 3 days pass without a workout, your twin drops one level. It never drops below Lv 1 Beginner. Rest check-ins still keep those days, but only a workout ends the tiredness.

What if my subscription ends?

Your progress pauses. Days without an active subscription don't count as missed, and your twin waits for you. Subscribe again and carry on where you left off.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Gym Twin and tap Restore Purchases, on the subscription screen or in Settings. Make sure your iPhone is signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with.

To manage or cancel your subscription, open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Back up first: in Gym Twin, open Settings and choose Back up with Apple. On the new iPhone, open Gym Twin, tap I have a backup and sign in with the same Apple ID.

Without a backup, progress can't be moved to a new phone.

What is Spot, and when does it send my data?

Spot is Gym Twin's AI coach. It also powers Food (calorie estimates from a meal photo) and AI-written programs. Nothing is sent until you tap Allow on the consent screen, and then only when you send a message, ask for a suggestion or program, or tap Estimate calories.

Requests go through our server to OpenAI. We don't store your messages or photos. Your chat, food log and programs stay on your phone. Apple Health values are sent only if you also turn on Share Apple Health values. Turn Spot off any time in Settings, then AI & privacy. Spot is not medical advice. Details are in the privacy policy.

Why is Spot, Food or Programs locked?

Spot, Food and AI programs are for ages 18 and up. Open Settings, then AI & privacy, then Your details and check your birth year. They also need a free trial or an active subscription, and your consent. If you are under 18, or haven't set a birth year, they stay off and the rest of Gym Twin works as usual.

How do friends and shared streaks work?

First choose Back up with Apple. Then open the Friends tab, tap Invite a friend and send the link or the 8-character code. It works once and expires after 7 days. When your friend accepts, you share a streak that counts a day when you both kept it.

Your friend sees your twin's name, look, level and whether you kept the day, and nothing else. You can leave, block or report from the streak screen. You can have up to 3 shared streaks.

What is Hardmode?

Hardmode is an optional mode with real stakes. Every workout needs a gym photo taken with the Gym Twin camera, and the photo stays on your phone. You earn 1.5x XP, but each missed day costs your twin a level. Miss a day at Level 1 and your twin dies: you start again at Level 1 with 0 XP. Freezes and shields don't work in Hardmode.

You can leave Hardmode any time; it takes effect after two days. If you are injured or ill, leave Hardmode and rest.

Apple Health isn't filling in my data

iOS only lets an app ask for Health access once. Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Gym Twin, and turn on the data types you want to share. You can always type values in yourself.

How do I delete my data?

In Gym Twin, open Settings, tap Delete my data and confirm. This deletes your account and progress from our servers and everything Gym Twin keeps on your phone, including your Spot chat, food log, programs and Hardmode photos. It can't be undone and it doesn't cancel your subscription.

Details are in the privacy policy.
