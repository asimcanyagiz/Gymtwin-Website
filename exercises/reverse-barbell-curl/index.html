How to do a reverse barbell curl

The reverse barbell curl mainly works your forearms and biceps. Pick the barbell up from a rack or the floor with your back straight, hands about shoulder-width apart, palms down. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight again.

  • Forearms
  • Biceps

Step by step

  1. Pick the barbell up from a rack or the floor with your back straight, hands about shoulder-width apart, palms down.
  2. Stand tall with the bar resting against your thighs and your arms straight.
  3. Keep your elbows close to your ribs and curl the bar up toward your chest.
  4. Pause at the top while keeping your wrists straight.
  5. Lower the bar slowly until your arms are straight again.

Form tips

  • Use a lighter bar than for a regular curl, because your arms are weaker in this position.
  • Keep your wrists straight, and use less weight if they bend back.
  • Without a barbell, curl dumbbells with your palms facing down.

Muscles worked

Forearms main
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.
Biceps main
The muscle on the front of your upper arm. It bends your elbow.

Equipment

You need barbell and plates, or ez curl bar.
