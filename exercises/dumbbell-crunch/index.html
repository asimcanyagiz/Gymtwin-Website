How to do a dumbbell crunch

The dumbbell crunch mainly works your core, with help from your obliques. Lie on your back on a mat or rug on a sturdy floor, with your knees bent and feet flat. Lower your shoulders back to the floor slowly.

  • Core
  • Obliques

Step by step

  1. Lie on your back on a mat or rug on a sturdy floor, with your knees bent and feet flat.
  2. Hold one dumbbell in both hands and rest it on your chest.
  3. Tighten your belly muscles and press your lower back toward the floor.
  4. Curl your head and shoulders up, bringing your ribs toward your hips.
  5. Pause for a moment at the top.
  6. Lower your shoulders back to the floor slowly.

Form tips

  • Start with a light dumbbell, and hold it firmly against your chest.
  • Keep a small gap between your chin and your chest, with your neck relaxed.
  • Without a dumbbell, do a Crunch with your arms crossed over your chest.

Muscles worked

Core main
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Obliques
The muscles on the sides of your waist. They twist and bend your torso.

Equipment

You need dumbbells.
