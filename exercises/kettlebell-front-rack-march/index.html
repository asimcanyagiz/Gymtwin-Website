How to do a kettlebell front rack march

The kettlebell front rack march mainly works your core and obliques, with help from your front shoulders, quads, glutes, and upper back. Bend your knees, keep your back straight, and lift one kettlebell by its handle. Lower the kettlebell to your side, bend your knees, and set it on the floor to switch hands.

  • Core

Step by step

  1. Bend your knees, keep your back straight, and lift one kettlebell by its handle.
  2. Bring it up to your shoulder, with your elbow close to your ribs.
  3. Stand tall on a sturdy, flat floor with your feet about hip-width apart.
  4. Tighten your belly muscles and keep the kettlebell steady against your chest.
  5. Lift one knee toward hip height, lower it, then lift the other knee and keep marching slowly.
  6. Lower the kettlebell to your side, bend your knees, and set it on the floor to switch hands.

Form tips

  • Use a light kettlebell, and keep clear space around you.
  • Keep your shoulders level so the kettlebell does not pull you sideways.
  • Without a kettlebell, hold a dumbbell upright against your chest.

Muscles worked

Core main
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Obliques main
The muscles on the sides of your waist. They twist and bend your torso.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Quads
The four muscles on the front of your thigh. They straighten your knee.
Glutes
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Upper back
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.

Equipment

You need kettlebells, or dumbbells.
