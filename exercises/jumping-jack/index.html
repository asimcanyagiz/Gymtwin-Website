How to do a jumping jack

The jumping jack mainly works your quads, glutes, and calves, with help from your side shoulders and core. Stand tall on a flat, non-slip floor with your feet together and your arms at your sides. Repeat at a steady pace.

  • Quads
  • Glutes
  • Calves
  • Side shoulders
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Stand tall on a flat, non-slip floor with your feet together and your arms at your sides.
  2. Jump your feet apart until they are a little wider than your shoulders.
  3. At the same time, swing your arms out to the sides and up above your head.
  4. Jump your feet back together as your arms swing down to your sides.
  5. Repeat at a steady pace.

Form tips

  • Land softly on the balls of your feet, with your knees slightly bent.
  • Keep your chest up and your neck relaxed, with your eyes ahead.
  • If jumping is too much, step one foot out to the side and back, then the other.

Muscles worked

Quads main
The four muscles on the front of your thigh. They straighten your knee.
Glutes main
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Calves main
The muscles on the back of your lower leg. They lift your heel.
Side shoulders
The side of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm out to the side.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
