How to do a bird dog row

The bird dog row mainly works your lats and upper back, with help from your rear shoulders, core, glutes, and lower back. Start on your hands and knees, with your hands under your shoulders and your knees under your hips. Lower your arm and leg to the floor, then repeat on the other side.

  • Lats
  • Upper back
  • Rear shoulders
  • Core
  • Glutes
  • Lower back

Step by step

  1. Start on your hands and knees, with your hands under your shoulders and your knees under your hips.
  2. Look at the floor and keep your back flat, like a tabletop.
  3. Reach one arm forward and the opposite leg back, both in line with your back.
  4. Bend that elbow and pull it back toward your ribs, like a row.
  5. Reach your arm forward again without letting your hips twist.
  6. Lower your arm and leg to the floor, then repeat on the other side.

Form tips

  • Keep your hips level and facing the floor, not tipping to the side.
  • Feel the muscles beside your shoulder blade pull your elbow back.
  • If balance is hard, keep your toes on the floor and only move your arm.

Muscles worked

Lats main
The wide muscles down the sides of your back. They pull your arms down and back.
Upper back main
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Rear shoulders
The back of your shoulder cap. It pulls your arm back and out.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Glutes
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Lower back
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
