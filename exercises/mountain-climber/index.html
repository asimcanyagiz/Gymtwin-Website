How to do a mountain climber

The mountain climber mainly works your core and quads, with help from your front shoulders, glutes, and obliques. Place your hands on a non-slip floor, about shoulder-width apart, with your arms straight. Keep switching at a pace you can hold without bouncing your hips.

  • Core
  • Quads
  • Front shoulders
  • Glutes
  • Obliques

Step by step

  1. Place your hands on a non-slip floor, about shoulder-width apart, with your arms straight.
  2. Walk your feet back so your body forms a straight line from head to heels.
  3. Tighten your belly muscles so your hips do not sag or rise.
  4. Pull one knee in toward your chest, keeping that foot just off the floor.
  5. Switch legs, sending one foot back as the other knee comes forward.
  6. Keep switching at a pace you can hold without bouncing your hips.

Form tips

  • Keep your shoulders over your hands and your eyes on the floor between them.
  • Stop and rest if your lower back sags or your wrists hurt.
  • If it is too hard, put your hands on a sturdy bench or step and slow down.

Muscles worked

Core main
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Quads main
The four muscles on the front of your thigh. They straighten your knee.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Glutes
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Obliques
The muscles on the sides of your waist. They twist and bend your torso.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
