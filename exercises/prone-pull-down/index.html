How to do a prone pull down

The prone pull down mainly works your lats and upper back, with help from your rear shoulders, lower back, and triceps. Lie face down on a mat with your arms stretched above your head, a little wider than your shoulders. Reach your arms back out above your head again, slowly, keeping your chest lifted.

  • Lats
  • Upper back
  • Rear shoulders
  • Lower back
  • Triceps

Step by step

  1. Lie face down on a mat with your arms stretched above your head, a little wider than your shoulders.
  2. Look at the floor and lift your chest and arms a little off the mat.
  3. Pull your elbows down toward your ribs, as if pulling a bar down to your chest.
  4. Squeeze the muscles between your shoulder blades at the bottom.
  5. Reach your arms back out above your head again, slowly, keeping your chest lifted.

Form tips

  • Keep your legs and hips resting on the floor as you pull.
  • Feel the sides of your back and your shoulder blades working as you pull.
  • If lifting your chest is too hard, keep it on the mat and just move your arms.

Muscles worked

Lats main
The wide muscles down the sides of your back. They pull your arms down and back.
Upper back main
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Rear shoulders
The back of your shoulder cap. It pulls your arm back and out.
Lower back
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.
Triceps
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
