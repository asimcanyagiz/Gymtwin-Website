How to do a crunch

The crunch mainly works your core, with help from your obliques. Lie on your back on a mat or rug on a sturdy floor, with your knees bent and feet flat. Pause for a moment, then lower your shoulders back to the floor slowly.

  • Core
  • Obliques

Step by step

  1. Lie on your back on a mat or rug on a sturdy floor, with your knees bent and feet flat.
  2. Cross your arms over your chest, or rest your fingertips lightly beside your ears.
  3. Tighten your belly muscles and press your lower back toward the floor.
  4. Curl your head and shoulders up until your shoulder blades leave the floor.
  5. Pause for a moment, then lower your shoulders back to the floor slowly.

Form tips

  • Lift with your belly muscles, not by pulling on your neck.
  • Keep a small gap between your chin and your chest.
  • Keep your feet flat and your lower back close to the floor.

Muscles worked

Core main
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Obliques
The muscles on the sides of your waist. They twist and bend your torso.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
