How to do a dumbbell hip thrust

The dumbbell hip thrust mainly works your glutes, with help from your hamstrings, quads, and core. Set a sturdy bench against a wall so it does not slide. Lower your hips slowly toward the floor.

  • Glutes
  • Hamstrings
  • Quads
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Set a sturdy bench against a wall so it does not slide.
  2. Sit in front of the bench with your upper back on its edge and your knees bent, feet flat.
  3. Rest a dumbbell across your hips with a folded towel under it, and hold it with both hands.
  4. Push through your heels to lift your hips until your body is one line from shoulders to knees.
  5. Squeeze your glutes at the top.
  6. Lower your hips slowly toward the floor.

Form tips

  • Keep your chin slightly tucked so your neck stays in line with your back.
  • Keep your belly muscles tight so your lower back does not arch at the top.
  • Start with a light dumbbell, or do a Glute Bridge if you have no bench.

Muscles worked

Glutes main
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Hamstrings
The muscles on the back of your thigh. They bend your knee and pull your leg back.
Quads
The four muscles on the front of your thigh. They straighten your knee.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

You need dumbbells and flat bench, or dumbbells and adjustable bench.
