How to do a cable wood chop

The cable wood chop mainly works your obliques and core, with help from your front shoulders, side shoulders, and glutes. Attach a handle to a high pulley and stand sideways to the machine. Slowly bring the handle back up to the start.

  • Obliques
  • Core
  • Front shoulders
  • Side shoulders
  • Glutes

Step by step

  1. Attach a handle to a high pulley and stand sideways to the machine.
  2. Place your feet about shoulder-width apart and bend your knees slightly.
  3. Reach up and hold the handle with both hands, with your arms nearly straight.
  4. Pull the handle down and across your body, turning your chest and watching your hands.
  5. Pause when your hands reach the hip or knee farthest from the machine.
  6. Slowly bring the handle back up to the start.

Form tips

  • Let your feet turn a little with your hips so your knees do not twist.
  • Start with a light weight, so you can control the return.
  • Without a cable machine, try a Crunch or a Side Plank instead.

Muscles worked

Obliques main
The muscles on the sides of your waist. They twist and bend your torso.
Core main
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Side shoulders
The side of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm out to the side.
Glutes
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.

Equipment

You need cable crossover.
