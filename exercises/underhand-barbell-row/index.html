How to do an underhand barbell row

The underhand barbell row mainly works your lats and upper back, with help from your biceps, rear shoulders, lower back, and forearms. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and the bar over the middle of your feet. Lower the bar until your arms are straight. When you finish, set it on the floor with a flat back.

  • Lats
  • Upper back
  • Biceps
  • Rear shoulders
  • Lower back
  • Forearms

Step by step

  1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and the bar over the middle of your feet.
  2. Bend your knees and push your hips back until you can reach the bar with a flat back.
  3. Grip the bar with your palms facing up, about shoulder-width apart.
  4. Push through your feet to lift the bar, keeping your arms straight and your back flat.
  5. Pull the bar toward your lower belly, keeping your elbows close to your sides.
  6. Lower the bar until your arms are straight. When you finish, set it on the floor with a flat back.

Form tips

  • Start with a light first set, and keep your back flat and your neck in line.
  • Keep your elbows brushing your sides as you pull.
  • Without a barbell, try a Dumbbell Bent Over Row instead.

Muscles worked

Lats main
The wide muscles down the sides of your back. They pull your arms down and back.
Upper back main
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Biceps
The muscle on the front of your upper arm. It bends your elbow.
Rear shoulders
The back of your shoulder cap. It pulls your arm back and out.
Lower back
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.
Forearms
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.

Equipment

You need barbell and plates.
