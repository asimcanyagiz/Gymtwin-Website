How to do a scapular push up

The scapular push up mainly works your upper back, with help from your chest, front shoulders, and core. Start in a push up position, with your hands under your shoulders and your arms straight. Keep moving slowly between these two positions, with your arms straight.

  • Upper back
  • Chest
  • Front shoulders
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Start in a push up position, with your hands under your shoulders and your arms straight.
  2. Keep your body in one straight line from head to heels.
  3. Without bending your elbows, let your chest sink as your shoulder blades squeeze together.
  4. Push the floor away so your shoulder blades spread apart and your upper back rises a little.
  5. Keep moving slowly between these two positions, with your arms straight.

Form tips

  • Move only your shoulder blades, and keep your elbows straight the whole time.
  • Keep your hips level, without letting them sag or rise.
  • If it is too hard, do it on your knees with your body straight from head to knees.

Muscles worked

Upper back main
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Chest
The broad muscle across the front of your ribcage. It pushes your arms forward and in.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
