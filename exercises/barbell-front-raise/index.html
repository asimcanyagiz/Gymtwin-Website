How to do a barbell front raise

The barbell front raise mainly works your front shoulders, with help from your side shoulders, chest, and core. Hold a barbell in front of your thighs, with your palms facing down and your hands shoulder-width apart. Lower the bar slowly back to your thighs.

  • Front shoulders
  • Side shoulders
  • Chest
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Hold a barbell in front of your thighs, with your palms facing down and your hands shoulder-width apart.
  2. Stand tall with your feet about hip-width apart and your chest up.
  3. Tighten your belly muscles and keep a small bend in your elbows.
  4. Raise the bar straight forward until it reaches about shoulder height.
  5. Pause for a moment at the top.
  6. Lower the bar slowly back to your thighs.

Form tips

  • Start with a light bar, and do not lean back to swing it up.
  • Keep your shoulders low, away from your ears.
  • Without a barbell, do a Dumbbell Front Raise instead.

Muscles worked

Front shoulders main
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Side shoulders
The side of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm out to the side.
Chest
The broad muscle across the front of your ribcage. It pushes your arms forward and in.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

You need barbell and plates.
