How to do a dumbbell side bend

The dumbbell side bend mainly works your obliques, with help from your core and forearms. Stand on a sturdy, flat floor with your feet about hip-width apart. Use the side of your waist away from the dumbbell to pull yourself up to standing.

  • Obliques
  • Core
  • Forearms

Step by step

  1. Stand on a sturdy, flat floor with your feet about hip-width apart.
  2. Hold a dumbbell in one hand by your side, and rest your other hand on your hip.
  3. Tighten your belly muscles so your upper body stays steady.
  4. Slowly bend sideways, sliding the dumbbell down your leg.
  5. Bend only as far as you can without leaning forward or back.
  6. Use the side of your waist away from the dumbbell to pull yourself up to standing.

Form tips

  • Start with a light dumbbell, so you can bend to the side without twisting.
  • Keep your hips still and your chest facing forward.
  • Without a dumbbell, do the same side bend with no weight in your hand.

Muscles worked

Obliques main
The muscles on the sides of your waist. They twist and bend your torso.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Forearms
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.

Equipment

You need dumbbells, or kettlebells.
