How to do a dumbbell russian twist

The dumbbell russian twist mainly works your obliques and core, with help from your lower back and forearms. Sit on a mat or rug on a sturdy, flat floor with your knees bent and your feet flat. Keep twisting from side to side with slow, steady moves.

  • Obliques
  • Core
  • Lower back
  • Forearms

Step by step

  1. Sit on a mat or rug on a sturdy, flat floor with your knees bent and your feet flat.
  2. Hold one dumbbell flat against your chest with both hands.
  3. Lean back a little, keeping your chest tall and your back straight.
  4. Twist your chest and the dumbbell to one side.
  5. Twist back past the middle to the other side.
  6. Keep twisting from side to side with slow, steady moves.

Form tips

  • Start with a light dumbbell, and stop twisting if your lower back rounds or hurts.
  • Turn your chest, not just your arms.
  • Without a dumbbell, clasp your hands together and twist the same way.

Muscles worked

Obliques main
The muscles on the sides of your waist. They twist and bend your torso.
Core main
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Lower back
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.
Forearms
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.

Equipment

You need dumbbells, or kettlebells, or medicine ball.
