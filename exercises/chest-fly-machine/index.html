How to do a chest fly machine

The chest fly machine mainly works your chest, with help from your front shoulders. Adjust the seat so the handles are about level with your chest. Open your arms slowly and stop when your elbows are even with your shoulders, not behind them.

  • Chest
  • Front shoulders

Step by step

  1. Adjust the seat so the handles are about level with your chest.
  2. Sit with your back flat against the pad and your feet flat on the floor.
  3. Hold the handles or rest your arms on the pads, with your elbows about level with your chest.
  4. Breathe out and squeeze your arms together in front of your chest in a smooth arc.
  5. Open your arms slowly and stop when your elbows are even with your shoulders, not behind them.

Form tips

  • Keep your back against the pad and your shoulders down, away from your ears.
  • Pick a weight that lets you open your arms slowly, with no jerking.
  • If the machine is not available, try a Dumbbell Fly or a Push Up.

Muscles worked

Chest main
The broad muscle across the front of your ribcage. It pushes your arms forward and in.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
