How to do a dumbbell dead bug

The dumbbell dead bug mainly works your core, with help from your obliques, front shoulders, and forearms. Lie on your back on a mat or rug on a sturdy, flat floor. Bring that leg back to the start, then switch legs and keep alternating slowly.

  • Core
  • Obliques
  • Front shoulders
  • Forearms

Step by step

  1. Lie on your back on a mat or rug on a sturdy, flat floor.
  2. Hold one dumbbell above your chest with both hands on its handle, and keep your arms straight.
  3. Lift your legs until your knees are over your hips and your shins are level with the floor.
  4. Press your lower back gently into the floor and tighten your belly muscles.
  5. Slowly straighten one leg toward the floor while the dumbbell stays still above your chest.
  6. Bring that leg back to the start, then switch legs and keep alternating slowly.

Form tips

  • Start with a light dumbbell, and keep it over your chest, never your face or neck.
  • Stop the leg before your lower back lifts off the floor.
  • Without a dumbbell, do the same leg moves with your arms straight up.

Muscles worked

Core main
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Obliques
The muscles on the sides of your waist. They twist and bend your torso.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Forearms
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.

Equipment

You need dumbbells.
