How to do a dumbbell triceps extension

The dumbbell triceps extension mainly works your triceps, with help from your core. Stand or sit tall, holding one dumbbell upright with both palms under its top weight. Straighten your elbows to raise the dumbbell back over your head.

  • Triceps
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Stand or sit tall, holding one dumbbell upright with both palms under its top weight.
  2. Raise the dumbbell over your head until your arms are straight.
  3. Tighten your belly muscles so your back does not arch, and keep your upper arms close to your ears.
  4. Bend your elbows to lower the dumbbell behind your head.
  5. Stop when your elbows are bent to about 90 degrees.
  6. Straighten your elbows to raise the dumbbell back over your head.

Form tips

  • Use a light dumbbell and a firm two-hand hold, since it passes close to your head.
  • Keep your elbows pointing forward and your head still, with your chin level.
  • Without a dumbbell, try a Bodyweight Triceps Extension.

Muscles worked

Triceps main
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

You need dumbbells.
