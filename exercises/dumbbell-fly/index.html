How to do a dumbbell fly

The dumbbell fly mainly works your chest, with help from your front shoulders and biceps. Sit on a sturdy flat bench, or on the floor, with a dumbbell resting on each thigh. Breathe out and squeeze your arms back together over your chest along the same arc.

  • Chest
  • Front shoulders
  • Biceps

Step by step

  1. Sit on a sturdy flat bench, or on the floor, with a dumbbell resting on each thigh.
  2. Lie back with your feet flat on the floor.
  3. Bring the dumbbells over your chest, with your palms facing each other and your elbows slightly bent.
  4. Breathe in and lower your arms out to the sides in a wide, smooth arc.
  5. Stop when your upper arms are about level with your chest.
  6. Breathe out and squeeze your arms back together over your chest along the same arc.

Form tips

  • Start with light dumbbells, because your arms are far from your body in this move.
  • Keep the same small bend in your elbows from start to finish, so it is not a press.
  • Without dumbbells, try a Push Up, and stop if you feel a sharp shoulder pull.

Muscles worked

Chest main
The broad muscle across the front of your ribcage. It pushes your arms forward and in.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Biceps
The muscle on the front of your upper arm. It bends your elbow.

Equipment

You need dumbbells and flat bench, or dumbbells and adjustable bench.
