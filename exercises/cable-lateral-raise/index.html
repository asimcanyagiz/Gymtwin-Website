How to do a cable lateral raise

The cable lateral raise mainly works your side shoulders, with help from your upper back and front shoulders. Set the cable pulley low and attach a single handle. Lower your arm slowly back to the start.

  • Side shoulders
  • Upper back
  • Front shoulders

Step by step

  1. Set the cable pulley low and attach a single handle.
  2. Stand sideways to the machine, holding the handle in the hand farthest from it.
  3. Stand tall with your arm down in front of you, and keep a small bend in your elbow.
  4. Raise your arm out to the side, leading with your elbow, until your hand is at shoulder height.
  5. Pause for a moment at the top.
  6. Lower your arm slowly back to the start.

Form tips

  • Hold a sturdy part of the machine with your free hand to stay steady.
  • Keep your shoulder low, away from your ear.
  • Without a cable machine, do a Dumbbell Lateral Raise instead.

Muscles worked

Side shoulders main
The side of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm out to the side.
Upper back
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.

Equipment

You need cable crossover.
