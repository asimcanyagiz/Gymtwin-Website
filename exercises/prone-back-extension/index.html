How to do a prone back extension

The prone back extension mainly works your lower back, with help from your glutes, hamstrings, and upper back. Lie face down on a mat with your legs straight and your feet hip-width apart. Lower your chest back to the mat slowly.

  • Lower back
  • Glutes
  • Hamstrings
  • Upper back

Step by step

  1. Lie face down on a mat with your legs straight and your feet hip-width apart.
  2. Rest your fingertips lightly beside your ears, with your elbows out to the sides.
  3. Look down at the floor so your neck stays in line with your back.
  4. Squeeze your lower back and butt muscles to lift your chest off the mat.
  5. Pause for a moment with your hips and legs pressed into the floor.
  6. Lower your chest back to the mat slowly.

Form tips

  • Lift your chest only about a hand's width off the floor.
  • Stop if you feel a pinch in your lower back.
  • If this is too hard, rest your arms on the floor by your sides.

Muscles worked

Lower back main
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.
Glutes
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Hamstrings
The muscles on the back of your thigh. They bend your knee and pull your leg back.
Upper back
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
