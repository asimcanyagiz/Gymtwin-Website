How to do a bear crawl

The bear crawl mainly works your core, front shoulders, and quads, with help from your triceps, chest, obliques, and glutes. Start on your hands and knees, with your hands under your shoulders and your knees under your hips. Keep taking small, steady steps, with your hips level and your knees just off the floor.

  • Core

Step by step

  1. Start on your hands and knees, with your hands under your shoulders and your knees under your hips.
  2. Tighten your belly muscles and keep your back flat and your neck in line with your spine.
  3. Press your hands into the floor and lift your knees just off the floor.
  4. Move your right hand and your left knee forward a short step at the same time.
  5. Move your left hand and your right knee forward the same way.
  6. Keep taking small, steady steps, with your hips level and your knees just off the floor.

Form tips

  • Keep your hips level, not rising up or swaying from side to side.
  • If it is too hard, rock gently forward and back on your hands and knees first.
  • Use a sturdy, non-slip surface with clear space ahead, such as a mat on a flat floor.

Muscles worked

Core main
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Front shoulders main
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Quads main
The four muscles on the front of your thigh. They straighten your knee.
Triceps
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Chest
The broad muscle across the front of your ribcage. It pushes your arms forward and in.
Obliques
The muscles on the sides of your waist. They twist and bend your torso.
Glutes
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
