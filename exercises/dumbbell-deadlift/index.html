How to do a dumbbell deadlift

The dumbbell deadlift mainly works your glutes, hamstrings, and lower back, with help from your quads, upper back, forearms, and core. Stand with your feet about hip-width apart, holding a dumbbell in each hand in front of your thighs. Push your feet into the floor and move your hips forward to stand tall.

  • Glutes
  • Hamstrings
  • Lower back
  • Quads
  • Upper back
  • Forearms
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Stand with your feet about hip-width apart, holding a dumbbell in each hand in front of your thighs.
  2. Tighten your belly muscles and keep your back flat.
  3. Push your hips back, as if closing a car door behind you with your bottom.
  4. Let your knees bend a little as the dumbbells slide down your legs.
  5. Lower to about the middle of your shins, or stop sooner if your back starts to round.
  6. Push your feet into the floor and move your hips forward to stand tall.

Form tips

  • Keep your neck in line with your back and look at the floor a little ahead.
  • Start with light dumbbells, and stop the set if your back rounds.
  • If you have no dumbbells, try a Glute Bridge.

Muscles worked

Glutes main
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Hamstrings main
The muscles on the back of your thigh. They bend your knee and pull your leg back.
Lower back main
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.
Quads
The four muscles on the front of your thigh. They straighten your knee.
Upper back
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Forearms
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

You need dumbbells, or kettlebells.
