Shoulders · Cable crossover

How to do a cable face pull

The cable face pull mainly works your rear shoulders and upper back, with help from your side shoulders and biceps. Set the cable pulley at about face height and attach a rope handle. Let your arms straighten slowly back to the start.

  • Rear shoulders
  • Upper back
  • Side shoulders
  • Biceps

Step by step

  1. Set the cable pulley at about face height and attach a rope handle.
  2. Hold an end of the rope in each hand, with your palms facing each other.
  3. Step back until the cable is tight and your arms are straight.
  4. Pull the rope toward your face, keeping your elbows high and out to the sides.
  5. Finish with your hands beside your ears, and squeeze your shoulder blades together.
  6. Let your arms straighten slowly back to the start.

Form tips

  • Stand tall and keep your neck long, without leaning back to pull.
  • Pick a light weight so your elbows can stay high.
  • Without a cable machine, try a Prone T Raise instead.

Muscles worked

Rear shoulders main
The back of your shoulder cap. It pulls your arm back and out.
Upper back main
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Side shoulders
The side of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm out to the side.
Biceps
The muscle on the front of your upper arm. It bends your elbow.

Equipment

You need cable crossover.
