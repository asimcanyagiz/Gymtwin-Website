How to do a standing calf raise

The standing calf raise mainly works your calves. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart and one hand on a wall or other sturdy support. Lower your heels slowly until they are back on the floor.

  • Calves

Step by step

  1. Stand tall with your feet hip-width apart and one hand on a wall or other sturdy support.
  2. Keep your knees straight but relaxed.
  3. Press through the balls of your feet and rise up onto your toes.
  4. Pause at the top and squeeze your calves.
  5. Lower your heels slowly until they are back on the floor.

Form tips

  • Rise straight up without letting your ankles roll in or out.
  • Lower slowly, because your calves still work on the way down.
  • Look straight ahead and keep your body tall, not leaning forward.

Muscles worked

Calves main
The muscles on the back of your lower leg. They lift your heel.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
