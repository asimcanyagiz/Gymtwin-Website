How to do a pike push up

The pike push up mainly works your front shoulders and side shoulders, with help from your triceps, chest, upper back, and core. Start in a push up position, with your hands about shoulder-width apart. Push your hands into the floor until your arms are straight again.

  • Front shoulders
  • Side shoulders
  • Triceps
  • Chest
  • Upper back
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Start in a push up position, with your hands about shoulder-width apart.
  2. Walk your feet toward your hands and lift your hips high, making an upside-down V.
  3. Straighten your arms and legs, and keep your head between your arms.
  4. Bend your elbows and lower your head toward the floor, keeping your neck in line with your back.
  5. Stop when your head is about a fist's width from the floor.
  6. Push your hands into the floor until your arms are straight again.

Form tips

  • Keep your hips high the whole time, so your shoulders do the work.
  • Angle your elbows back, not straight out to the sides.
  • If it is too hard, bend your elbows only a little and build up over time.

Muscles worked

Front shoulders main
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Side shoulders main
The side of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm out to the side.
Triceps
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Chest
The broad muscle across the front of your ribcage. It pushes your arms forward and in.
Upper back
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
