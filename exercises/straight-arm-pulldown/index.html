Back · Cable crossover

How to do a straight arm pulldown

The straight arm pulldown mainly works your lats, with help from your triceps, core, and rear shoulders. Stand facing a cable machine set high, with a bar attached. Let the bar rise slowly along the same arc until it is about head height.

  • Lats
  • Triceps
  • Core
  • Rear shoulders

Step by step

  1. Stand facing a cable machine set high, with a bar attached.
  2. Grab the bar with your palms facing down and your hands about shoulder-width apart.
  3. Step back until the cable is tight.
  4. Lean forward a little with a flat back, keeping your head in line with it.
  5. Keep your arms nearly straight and pull the bar down in an arc toward your thighs.
  6. Let the bar rise slowly along the same arc until it is about head height.

Form tips

  • Keep your elbows almost straight the whole time, and do not bend them to pull.
  • Feel the sides of your back pull the bar down, not your hands.
  • Without a cable machine, try a Prone Pull Down on the floor.

Muscles worked

Lats main
The wide muscles down the sides of your back. They pull your arms down and back.
Triceps
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Rear shoulders
The back of your shoulder cap. It pulls your arm back and out.

Equipment

You need cable crossover, or lat pulldown.
