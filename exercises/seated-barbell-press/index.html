How to do a seated barbell press

The seated barbell press mainly works your front shoulders and side shoulders, with help from your triceps and upper back. Set a sturdy bench in a rack, with the bar above your head and safety bars just below chin height. Rest the bar back on the rack when you finish.

  • Front shoulders
  • Side shoulders
  • Triceps
  • Upper back

Step by step

  1. Set a sturdy bench in a rack, with the bar above your head and safety bars just below chin height.
  2. Sit with your back and hips firmly against the bench and your feet flat on the floor.
  3. Grip the bar just outside your shoulders and lift it off the rack.
  4. Lower the bar slowly to about chin height, keeping your forearms upright.
  5. Press the bar straight up until your arms are almost straight.
  6. Rest the bar back on the rack when you finish.

Form tips

  • Start with a light first set, and use a spotter or safety bars.
  • Keep your back on the pad and your eyes straight ahead.
  • Without safety bars or a spotter, use a lighter weight or try a Dumbbell Overhead Press.

Muscles worked

Front shoulders main
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Side shoulders main
The side of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm out to the side.
Triceps
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Upper back
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.

Equipment

You need barbell and plates and adjustable bench, or smith machine and adjustable bench.
