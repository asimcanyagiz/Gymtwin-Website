How to do a dumbbell row

The dumbbell row mainly works your lats and upper back, with help from your biceps, rear shoulders, forearms, and core. Place one hand and the same-side knee on a sturdy bench, with your other foot on the floor. Lower the dumbbell slowly until your arm is straight again.

  • Lats
  • Upper back
  • Biceps
  • Rear shoulders
  • Forearms
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Place one hand and the same-side knee on a sturdy bench, with your other foot on the floor.
  2. Hold a dumbbell in your free hand, with your arm hanging straight down under your shoulder.
  3. Keep your back flat and look at the floor just ahead of your hand.
  4. Pull the dumbbell up toward your hip, keeping your elbow close to your side.
  5. Pause when your elbow is about level with your back.
  6. Lower the dumbbell slowly until your arm is straight again.

Form tips

  • Keep your shoulders level instead of twisting your chest up as you pull.
  • Start with a light dumbbell, and check that the bench is steady before you lift.
  • Without a bench, rest your free hand on a sturdy chair or table instead.

Muscles worked

Lats main
The wide muscles down the sides of your back. They pull your arms down and back.
Upper back main
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Biceps
The muscle on the front of your upper arm. It bends your elbow.
Rear shoulders
The back of your shoulder cap. It pulls your arm back and out.
Forearms
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

You need dumbbells.
