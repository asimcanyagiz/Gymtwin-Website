How to do a dumbbell overhead press

The dumbbell overhead press mainly works your front shoulders and side shoulders, with help from your triceps, upper back, and core. Stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart, holding a dumbbell in each hand. Lower the dumbbells slowly back to your shoulders.

  • Front shoulders
  • Side shoulders
  • Triceps
  • Upper back
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart, holding a dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Raise the dumbbells to your shoulders, with your palms facing forward.
  3. Tighten your belly muscles and keep your ribs down.
  4. Press the dumbbells straight up until your arms are almost straight.
  5. Pause with the dumbbells right above your shoulders.
  6. Lower the dumbbells slowly back to your shoulders.

Form tips

  • Start with a light pair, and use a lighter pair if your lower back starts to arch.
  • Look straight ahead and keep your neck relaxed as the dumbbells pass your face.
  • Without dumbbells, try a Pike Push Up, with your hips high in an upside-down V.

Muscles worked

Front shoulders main
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Side shoulders main
The side of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm out to the side.
Triceps
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Upper back
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

You need dumbbells.
