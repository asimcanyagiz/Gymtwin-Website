How to do a barbell deadlift

The barbell deadlift mainly works your glutes, hamstrings, and lower back, with help from your quads, upper back, forearms, and core. Stand with your feet about hip-width apart and the bar over the middle of your feet. Push your hips back, then bend your knees to lower the bar to the floor, keeping your back flat.

  • Glutes
  • Hamstrings
  • Lower back
  • Quads
  • Upper back
  • Forearms
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Stand with your feet about hip-width apart and the bar over the middle of your feet.
  2. Bend your knees and push your hips back until you can grip the bar just outside your legs.
  3. Tighten your belly muscles and lift your chest so your back is flat.
  4. Push your feet into the floor and stand up, keeping the bar close to your legs.
  5. Stand tall at the top with your hips and knees straight, without leaning back.
  6. Push your hips back, then bend your knees to lower the bar to the floor, keeping your back flat.

Form tips

  • Keep the bar touching or very close to your legs the whole time.
  • Keep your neck in line with your back, and stop the set if your back rounds.
  • Start with a light bar, or try a Dumbbell Deadlift if you have no barbell.

Muscles worked

Glutes main
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Hamstrings main
The muscles on the back of your thigh. They bend your knee and pull your leg back.
Lower back main
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.
Quads
The four muscles on the front of your thigh. They straighten your knee.
Upper back
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Forearms
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

You need barbell and plates.
