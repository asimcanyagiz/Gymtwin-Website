How to do a burpee

The burpee mainly works your quads, glutes, and chest, with help from your front shoulders, triceps, core, and hamstrings. Stand tall with your feet about shoulder-width apart and your arms at your sides. Push through your feet to stand, or jump straight up, and reach your arms up toward the ceiling.

Step by step

  1. Stand tall with your feet about shoulder-width apart and your arms at your sides.
  2. Bend your knees and hips, and reach down to place both hands on the floor in front of your feet.
  3. Jump or step your feet back into a push-up position, in one straight line from head to heels.
  4. Bend your elbows to lower your chest toward the floor, then push back up until your arms are straight.
  5. Jump or step your feet forward, so they land softly near your hands with your knees bent.
  6. Push through your feet to stand, or jump straight up, and reach your arms up toward the ceiling.

Form tips

  • Tighten your belly muscles and look just past your hands, so your hips do not sag.
  • If it is too hard, step instead of jumping and put your knees down for the push-up.
  • Use a sturdy, non-slip surface with clear space around you, such as a mat on a flat floor.

Muscles worked

Quads main
The four muscles on the front of your thigh. They straighten your knee.
Glutes main
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Chest main
The broad muscle across the front of your ribcage. It pushes your arms forward and in.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Triceps
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Hamstrings
The muscles on the back of your thigh. They bend your knee and pull your leg back.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
