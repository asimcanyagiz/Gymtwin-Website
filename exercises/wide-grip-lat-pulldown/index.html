How to do a wide grip lat pulldown

The wide grip lat pulldown mainly works your lats, with help from your biceps, upper back, rear shoulders, and forearms. Sit at the pulldown station and set the thigh pad so it holds your legs down. Let the bar rise slowly until your arms are straight again.

  • Lats
  • Biceps
  • Upper back
  • Rear shoulders
  • Forearms

Step by step

  1. Sit at the pulldown station and set the thigh pad so it holds your legs down.
  2. Grip the bar with your palms facing away from you, wider than your shoulders.
  3. Lean back a little and lift your chest, with your arms straight above you.
  4. Pull your elbows down and toward your sides, bringing the bar to your upper chest.
  5. Squeeze your shoulder blades down and together at the bottom.
  6. Let the bar rise slowly until your arms are straight again.

Form tips

  • Pull with your elbows, and keep your chin level instead of pushing your head forward.
  • Choose a weight light enough that the thigh pad keeps you seated.
  • Without a pulldown machine, try a Prone Pull Down on the floor.

Muscles worked

Lats main
The wide muscles down the sides of your back. They pull your arms down and back.
Biceps
The muscle on the front of your upper arm. It bends your elbow.
Upper back
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Rear shoulders
The back of your shoulder cap. It pulls your arm back and out.
Forearms
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.

Equipment

You need lat pulldown, or cable crossover.
