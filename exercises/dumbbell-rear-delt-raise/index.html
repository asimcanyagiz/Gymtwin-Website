How to do a dumbbell rear delt raise

The dumbbell rear delt raise mainly works your rear shoulders, with help from your upper back, side shoulders, and lower back. Stand with your feet about hip-width apart and your knees a little bent, holding a dumbbell in each hand. Lower the dumbbells slowly back down.

  • Rear shoulders
  • Upper back
  • Side shoulders
  • Lower back

Step by step

  1. Stand with your feet about hip-width apart and your knees a little bent, holding a dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Push your hips back and lean forward with a straight back until your chest points toward the floor.
  3. Let the dumbbells hang below your chest, with a small bend in your elbows.
  4. Raise your arms out to the sides until your elbows are about level with your back.
  5. Squeeze your shoulder blades together for a moment.
  6. Lower the dumbbells slowly back down.

Form tips

  • Look at the floor a little ahead of your hands to keep your neck long.
  • Keep your back flat, and use a light pair so you do not need to swing.
  • Without dumbbells, try a Prone T Raise instead.

Muscles worked

Rear shoulders main
The back of your shoulder cap. It pulls your arm back and out.
Upper back
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Side shoulders
The side of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm out to the side.
Lower back
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.

Equipment

You need dumbbells.
