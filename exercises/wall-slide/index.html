How to do a wall slide

The wall slide mainly works your side shoulders and upper back, with help from your rear shoulders and front shoulders. Stand with your back against a wall and your feet a short step away from it. Slide your arms back down to the W shape, keeping them on the wall.

  • Side shoulders
  • Upper back
  • Rear shoulders
  • Front shoulders

Step by step

  1. Stand with your back against a wall and your feet a short step away from it.
  2. Bend your elbows and press your forearms and hands against the wall, in a W shape.
  3. Tighten your belly muscles lightly so your lower back does not arch.
  4. Slide your arms up the wall until they are almost straight, making a Y shape.
  5. Slide your arms back down to the W shape, keeping them on the wall.

Form tips

  • Go only as high as your arms and hands can stay on the wall.
  • Keep your shoulders low, away from your ears, as you slide.
  • Move slowly, and keep your neck relaxed.

Muscles worked

Side shoulders main
The side of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm out to the side.
Upper back main
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Rear shoulders
The back of your shoulder cap. It pulls your arm back and out.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
