How to do a plank

The plank mainly works your core, with help from your obliques, front shoulders, glutes, and lower back. Lie face down on a mat or rug on a sturdy floor, with your elbows under your shoulders. Hold this position and keep breathing steadily.

  • Core
  • Obliques
  • Front shoulders
  • Glutes
  • Lower back

Step by step

  1. Lie face down on a mat or rug on a sturdy floor, with your elbows under your shoulders.
  2. Press up onto your forearms and toes, so your knees and hips leave the floor.
  3. Make one straight line from your head to your heels.
  4. Tighten your belly muscles and squeeze your glutes.
  5. Look at the floor just past your hands to keep your neck in line with your back.
  6. Hold this position and keep breathing steadily.

Form tips

  • Check that your hips stay level with your shoulders and heels.
  • Press your forearms into the floor so your shoulders do not sink.
  • Stop and lower your knees if your lower back sags or pinches.

Muscles worked

Core main
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.
Obliques
The muscles on the sides of your waist. They twist and bend your torso.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Glutes
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Lower back
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
