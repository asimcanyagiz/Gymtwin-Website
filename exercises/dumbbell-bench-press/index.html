How to do a dumbbell bench press

The dumbbell bench press mainly works your chest, with help from your triceps and front shoulders. Sit on a sturdy flat bench with a dumbbell resting on each thigh. When you finish, bring the dumbbells to your thighs and sit up.

  • Chest
  • Triceps
  • Front shoulders

Step by step

  1. Sit on a sturdy flat bench with a dumbbell resting on each thigh.
  2. Lie back and bring the dumbbells to the sides of your chest, with your feet flat on the floor.
  3. Pull your shoulder blades back and down against the bench.
  4. Press the dumbbells up until your arms are straight over your chest.
  5. Lower them slowly until your elbows are about level with the bench, angled back about 45 degrees.
  6. When you finish, bring the dumbbells to your thighs and sit up.

Form tips

  • Without a spotter, use dumbbells light enough to control all the way up and down.
  • Keep your head resting on the bench and your feet flat on the floor.
  • If you have no bench, try a Dumbbell Floor Press.

Muscles worked

Chest main
The broad muscle across the front of your ribcage. It pushes your arms forward and in.
Triceps
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.

Equipment

You need dumbbells and flat bench, or dumbbells and adjustable bench.
