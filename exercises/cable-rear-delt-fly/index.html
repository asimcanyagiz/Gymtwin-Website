How to do a cable rear delt fly

The cable rear delt fly mainly works your rear shoulders, with help from your upper back. Set both cable pulleys at about shoulder height and attach a single handle to each. Bring your hands back together slowly, keeping the same small bend in your elbows.

  • Rear shoulders
  • Upper back

Step by step

  1. Set both cable pulleys at about shoulder height and attach a single handle to each.
  2. Reach across and take the left handle in your right hand and the right handle in your left.
  3. Step back to the middle, with your arms out in front and your elbows slightly bent.
  4. Pull your hands apart in a wide arc until your arms are in line with your shoulders.
  5. Squeeze your shoulder blades together for a moment.
  6. Bring your hands back together slowly, keeping the same small bend in your elbows.

Form tips

  • Keep your chest up and your back still, and move only your arms.
  • Pick a light weight so you do not need to lean back to pull.
  • Without a cable machine, try a Prone T Raise instead.

Muscles worked

Rear shoulders main
The back of your shoulder cap. It pulls your arm back and out.
Upper back
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.

Equipment

You need cable crossover, or pec deck.
