How to do a single leg glute bridge

The single leg glute bridge mainly works your glutes, with help from your hamstrings, lower back, and core. Lie on your back with both knees bent and your feet flat on the floor. Switch legs and repeat on the other side.

  • Glutes
  • Hamstrings
  • Lower back
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Lie on your back with both knees bent and your feet flat on the floor.
  2. Lift one foot and straighten that leg, keeping it in line with your other thigh.
  3. Push the heel on the floor down and lift your hips.
  4. Stop when your body is one line from your shoulders to the knee of your pushing leg.
  5. Lower your hips slowly until they almost touch the floor.
  6. Switch legs and repeat on the other side.

Form tips

  • Keep both hips level so they do not twist or drop to one side.
  • Feel it mostly in the glute of the leg that is on the floor.
  • If it is too hard, try the Glute Bridge with both feet on the floor.

Muscles worked

Glutes main
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Hamstrings
The muscles on the back of your thigh. They bend your knee and pull your leg back.
Lower back
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
