How to do a glute bridge

The glute bridge mainly works your glutes, with help from your hamstrings, lower back, and core. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your arms by your sides. Lower your hips slowly back to the floor.

  • Glutes
  • Hamstrings
  • Lower back
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your arms by your sides.
  2. Place your feet flat on the floor, about hip-width apart, with your heels under your knees.
  3. Push your heels down and lift your hips until your body is one line from shoulders to knees.
  4. Squeeze your glutes at the top.
  5. Lower your hips slowly back to the floor.

Form tips

  • Keep your belly muscles tight so your lower back does not arch.
  • Feel the work mostly in your glutes and the back of your thighs.
  • Rest your head on the floor and keep your neck relaxed.

Muscles worked

Glutes main
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Hamstrings
The muscles on the back of your thigh. They bend your knee and pull your leg back.
Lower back
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
