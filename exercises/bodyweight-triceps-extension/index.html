How to do a bodyweight triceps extension

The bodyweight triceps extension mainly works your triceps, with help from your front shoulders and core. Place your hands on the edge of a sturdy bench, table, or counter, about shoulder-width apart. Straighten your elbows to push your body back to the start.

  • Triceps
  • Front shoulders
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Place your hands on the edge of a sturdy bench, table, or counter, about shoulder-width apart.
  2. Walk your feet back until your body makes one straight, slanted line from head to heels.
  3. Tighten your belly muscles so your hips do not sag.
  4. Bend your elbows to lower your forehead toward your hands, keeping your elbows pointing forward.
  5. Stop when your head is just above your hands.
  6. Straighten your elbows to push your body back to the start.

Form tips

  • Keep your neck in line with your back as your head lowers.
  • Use a higher surface to make this easier, or a lower one to make it harder.
  • Check that the surface is firm and will not slide or tip before you lean on it.

Muscles worked

Triceps main
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
