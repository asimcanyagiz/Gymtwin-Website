How to do a barbell back squat

The barbell back squat mainly works your quads and glutes, with help from your hamstrings, lower back, and core. Set the bar at upper-chest height. Step under it so it rests on your upper back, not your neck. Push through your whole foot to stand up, and breathe out near the top.

  • Quads
  • Glutes
  • Hamstrings
  • Lower back
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Set the bar at upper-chest height. Step under it so it rests on your upper back, not your neck.
  2. Hold the bar just outside your shoulders, stand up to lift it off, and take a small step back.
  3. Place your feet about shoulder-width apart, with your toes turned out a little.
  4. Breathe in and tighten your belly muscles. Sit down and back, bending your hips and knees together.
  5. Lower until your thighs are about parallel to the floor, or as far as you can keep your back straight.
  6. Push through your whole foot to stand up, and breathe out near the top.

Form tips

  • Keep your chest up and your back straight, not rounded.
  • Push your knees out in line with your toes.
  • Without safety bars or a spotter, use a lighter weight or try a Bodyweight Squat.

Muscles worked

Quads main
The four muscles on the front of your thigh. They straighten your knee.
Glutes main
The muscles of your butt. They straighten your hips and push you up from a squat.
Hamstrings
The muscles on the back of your thigh. They bend your knee and pull your leg back.
Lower back
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

You need barbell and plates and squat rack, or smith machine.
