How to do a dumbbell wrist curl

The dumbbell wrist curl mainly works your forearms. Sit on a sturdy bench or chair with a dumbbell in one hand, palm facing up. Switch hands to work the other forearm.

  • Forearms

Step by step

  1. Sit on a sturdy bench or chair with a dumbbell in one hand, palm facing up.
  2. Rest your forearm on your thigh, with your wrist and hand hanging past your knee.
  3. Let your wrist bend down so the dumbbell lowers slowly toward the floor.
  4. Curl your hand back up by bending only your wrist.
  5. Switch hands to work the other forearm.

Form tips

  • Keep your forearm resting on your thigh so only your wrist moves.
  • Use a light dumbbell and hold it firmly, because wrist muscles tire fast.
  • Without a dumbbell, hold a water bottle the same way.

Muscles worked

Forearms main
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.

Equipment

You need dumbbells.
