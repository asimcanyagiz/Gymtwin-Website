How to do a self resisted biceps curl

The self resisted biceps curl mainly works your biceps, with help from your forearms. Sit or stand tall, with one arm down at your side and your palm facing up. Switch arms to work the other side.

  • Biceps
  • Forearms

Step by step

  1. Sit or stand tall, with one arm down at your side and your palm facing up.
  2. Place your other hand on top of your wrist.
  3. Curl your hand toward your shoulder while your top hand pushes down against your wrist.
  4. Pause at the top while your top hand keeps pushing down.
  5. Lower your hand slowly while your top hand keeps pushing down.
  6. Switch arms to work the other side.

Form tips

  • Push down just hard enough that the curl stays slow and smooth.
  • Keep your elbow close to your side, your shoulder down and your wrist straight.
  • Use the same amount of push on both sides.

Muscles worked

Biceps main
The muscle on the front of your upper arm. It bends your elbow.
Forearms
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
