How to do a lying leg curl machine

The lying leg curl machine mainly works your hamstrings, with help from your calves. Lie face down on the bench with your knees at its edge. Lower the pad slowly until your legs are almost straight.

  • Hamstrings
  • Calves

Step by step

  1. Lie face down on the bench with your knees at its edge.
  2. Set the roller pad on the back of your lower legs, just above your heels.
  3. Hold the handles and press your hips into the bench.
  4. Bend your knees to bring the pad toward your glutes.
  5. Squeeze the backs of your thighs for a moment.
  6. Lower the pad slowly until your legs are almost straight.

Form tips

  • Keep your hips down on the bench instead of lifting them to move the weight.
  • Move slowly and smoothly the whole way.
  • Start light, or try a Glute Bridge if your gym has no leg curl machine.

Muscles worked

Hamstrings main
The muscles on the back of your thigh. They bend your knee and pull your leg back.
Calves
The muscles on the back of your lower leg. They lift your heel.

Equipment

You need leg curl.
