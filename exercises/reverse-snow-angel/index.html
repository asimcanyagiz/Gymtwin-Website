How to do a reverse snow angel

The reverse snow angel mainly works your upper back and rear shoulders, with help from your lower back, lats, and side shoulders. Lie face down on a mat with your arms by your sides and your thumbs pointing to the ceiling. Sweep your arms back down to your sides along the same path.

  • Upper back
  • Rear shoulders
  • Lower back
  • Lats
  • Side shoulders

Step by step

  1. Lie face down on a mat with your arms by your sides and your thumbs pointing to the ceiling.
  2. Look down and lift your chest and arms a little off the floor.
  3. Keep your arms straight and thumbs up as you sweep them out to the sides and above your head.
  4. Reach your hands as far above your head as you can without lifting your chest higher.
  5. Sweep your arms back down to your sides along the same path.

Form tips

  • Keep your arms moving slowly and your chest lifted the whole time.
  • Stop if you feel a pinch in your lower back, and lift your chest less.
  • If the full arc is hard, make the arc smaller and keep your elbows straight.

Muscles worked

Upper back main
The muscles around your shoulder blades. They pull your shoulders back and steady them.
Rear shoulders main
The back of your shoulder cap. It pulls your arm back and out.
Lower back
The muscles along your spine at waist height. They keep your back straight as you lift.
Lats
The wide muscles down the sides of your back. They pull your arms down and back.
Side shoulders
The side of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm out to the side.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
