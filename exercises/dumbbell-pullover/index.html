How to do a dumbbell pullover

The dumbbell pullover mainly works your lats and chest, with help from your triceps and core. Lie back on a sturdy bench, with your head resting on it and your feet flat on the floor. Pull the dumbbell back over your chest along the same arc.

  • Lats
  • Chest
  • Triceps
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Lie back on a sturdy bench, with your head resting on it and your feet flat on the floor.
  2. Hold one end of the dumbbell in both hands, with your arms straight over your chest.
  3. Bend your elbows a little and keep them that way.
  4. Lower the dumbbell in an arc behind your head until you feel a gentle stretch along your sides.
  5. Pull the dumbbell back over your chest along the same arc.

Form tips

  • Start with a light dumbbell, and ask a spotter to hand it to you if one is nearby.
  • Keep your ribs down so your lower back does not arch as the dumbbell lowers.
  • Without a bench, lie on the floor with bent knees and stop when your arms touch it.

Muscles worked

Lats main
The wide muscles down the sides of your back. They pull your arms down and back.
Chest main
The broad muscle across the front of your ribcage. It pushes your arms forward and in.
Triceps
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

You need dumbbells and flat bench, or dumbbells and adjustable bench.
