How to do a barbell skull crusher

The barbell skull crusher mainly works your triceps, with help from your front shoulders and forearms. Lie on a sturdy bench with your head and back resting on it and your feet flat on the floor. Straighten your elbows to push the bar back over your chest.

  • Triceps
  • Front shoulders
  • Forearms

Step by step

  1. Lie on a sturdy bench with your head and back resting on it and your feet flat on the floor.
  2. Have a spotter hand you the barbell, with your hands about shoulder-width apart and your arms straight over your chest.
  3. Keep your upper arms still, pointing straight up.
  4. Bend only your elbows to lower the bar slowly toward your forehead.
  5. Stop when the bar is about a hand's width above your forehead.
  6. Straighten your elbows to push the bar back over your chest.

Form tips

  • Use a light weight for your first set, and stop lowering if your elbows start to drift.
  • Keep your elbows pointing at the ceiling, not out to the sides.
  • Without a spotter, try a Dumbbell Triceps Extension instead.

Muscles worked

Triceps main
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Forearms
The muscles between your elbow and wrist. They power your grip and wrist.

Equipment

You need barbell and plates and flat bench, or barbell and plates and adjustable bench, or ez curl bar and flat bench, or ez curl bar and adjustable bench.
