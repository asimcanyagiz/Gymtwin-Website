How to do a pause push up

The pause push up mainly works your chest, with help from your front shoulders, triceps, and core. Place your hands on the floor a little wider than your shoulders, with your arms straight. Push the floor away until your arms are straight again.

  • Chest
  • Front shoulders
  • Triceps
  • Core

Step by step

  1. Place your hands on the floor a little wider than your shoulders, with your arms straight.
  2. Step your feet back until your body is one straight line from head to heels.
  3. Squeeze your glutes and tighten your belly muscles.
  4. Lower your chest until it is about a fist's width from the floor, with your elbows angled back.
  5. Hold still there for a moment, and keep breathing.
  6. Push the floor away until your arms are straight again.

Form tips

  • Keep your chest just above the floor during the hold, not resting on it.
  • Keep your belly muscles tight so your hips do not sag while you hold.
  • If the hold is too hard, make it shorter or try a Knee Push Up.

Muscles worked

Chest main
The broad muscle across the front of your ribcage. It pushes your arms forward and in.
Front shoulders
The front of your shoulder cap. It lifts your arm forward and helps in every press.
Triceps
The muscle on the back of your upper arm. It straightens your elbow.
Core
The belly muscles at the front of your trunk. They keep your torso steady.

Equipment

No equipment, just your body weight.
