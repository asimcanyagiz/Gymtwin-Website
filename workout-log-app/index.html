Workout log

A simple workout log for iPhone

Gym Twin logs a workout two ways. A quick log takes about 10 seconds: pick the body regions you trained, set the time and say how it felt. A live workout tracks every set, rep and weight as you go, with a rest timer between sets. Either one keeps your daily gym streak.

Live workouts

Start a session from a plan or from scratch and log sets, weight and reps as you lift. A rest timer runs between sets and shows on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island as a Live Activity. If the app closes mid-workout, you pick up where you left off.

Quick logs

Trained somewhere you don't want to tap through every set? Log the session in about 10 seconds: body regions, minutes and how hard it felt.

An exercise library that works offline

More than 90 exercises with step-by-step form guides, sorted by body region and equipment. The same guides are on this site in the exercise library.

A log that keeps you coming back

Every workout feeds your streak and your pixel twin, which grows through five levels as you earn XP from the minutes you train. A Home Screen heat map shows the weeks you trained.

Try it in Gym Twin

Plan your gym days, keep the streak and watch your pixel twin grow, from Beginner to Beast Mode.
