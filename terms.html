How we deal with illegal content

We work to keep illegal content out of Gym Twins and to act fast when it shows up. This is how:

  • Little room for it. You can only show a short twin name. There are no messages, pictures, links or search, and invites are single-use and expire after 7 days.
  • Automatic checks. Twin names are checked on our server with a keyword list and pattern checks. The keyword list ignores capital letters, accents and look-alike characters. The patterns look for things like web links, email addresses, @handles and phone numbers. If a name fails a check, it is replaced with "Unnamed twin". These checks are automatic and can make mistakes. If one got your name wrong, you can appeal (see Reports, complaints and appeals).
  • Reports. Anyone can tell us about a name or behavior that looks illegal or breaks these Terms. We aim to look at each report within 24 hours, and we remove or replace illegal content quickly once we know about it.
  • What we can do. We can replace a twin name with "Unnamed twin", remove a twin from a shared streak, end a shared streak, and suspend or ban an account. We remove accounts that we have good reason to link to a terrorist group that is banned in the UK.
  • Serious cases. We report content that shows or promotes the sexual abuse or exploitation of children to the authorities, as the law requires (in the UK, to the National Crime Agency). We may report other serious crimes to the police.

Hardmode

Hardmode is an optional, harder way to play. Only turn it on if you understand the rules:

  • Every workout needs a gym photo taken with the Gym Twins camera. The photo stays on your phone. Take photos only of yourself or your own surroundings and respect other people's privacy in the gym.
  • You earn 1.5x XP on workouts, and a Hardmode frame for your twin.
  • If you miss a Hardmode day, your twin loses a level, whatever your streak is. Streak freezes and level shields don't work in Hardmode.
  • If you miss a day at Level 1, your twin dies. You start again at Level 1 with 0 XP and a 0 streak, and Hardmode ends. A death is decided by our server and confirmed about 48 hours after the day ends. Your gems, medals and achievements stay.
  • You can leave Hardmode at any time. It takes effect after two days.
  • While your subscription is inactive or our server can't confirm it, no death is recorded and the verdict waits. If our server makes a mistake, we can reverse a verdict.

Hardmode affects a game character only. It is never a reason to train through pain, illness or injury, to skip rest, or to risk your safety. If you can't train safely, leave Hardmode.
