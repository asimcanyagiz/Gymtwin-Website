  • You can leave a shared streak, block a friend or report a twin name or behavior at any time. We may review reports, end a shared streak, remove content or suspend an account.
  • A shared streak counts a day only when both of you kept it. If one of you leaves, deletes their account or stops subscribing, the shared streak can end or pause, and we can't restore it.

Hardmode

Hardmode is an optional, harder way to play. Only turn it on if you understand the rules:

  • Every workout needs a gym photo taken with the Gym Twin camera. The photo stays on your phone. Take photos only of yourself or your own surroundings and respect other people's privacy in the gym.
  • You earn 1.5x XP on workouts, and a Hardmode frame for your twin.
  • If you miss a Hardmode day, your twin loses a level, whatever your streak is. Streak freezes and level shields don't work in Hardmode.
  • If you miss a day at Level 1, your twin dies. You start again at Level 1 with 0 XP and a 0 streak, and Hardmode ends. A death is decided by our server and confirmed about 48 hours after the day ends. Your gems, medals and achievements stay.
  • You can leave Hardmode at any time. It takes effect after two days.
  • While your subscription is inactive or our server can't confirm it, no death is recorded and the verdict waits. If our server makes a mistake, we can reverse a verdict.

Hardmode affects a game character only. It is never a reason to train through pain, illness or injury, to skip rest, or to risk your safety. If you can't train safely, leave Hardmode.
