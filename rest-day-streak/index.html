Rest days

Do rest days break your workout streak?

In many streak apps, yes: they count only days with a workout, so every rest day resets you. In Gym Twin, no. Any day that isn't in your training plan is a rest day, and one tap on Rest check-in keeps the streak alive. A workout on a rest day counts too.

Gym days and rest days

You choose your gym days once, for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday. On those days the streak needs a workout of 10 minutes or more. Every other day is a rest day, and a check-in is all it needs. A check-in alone doesn't keep a gym day, which is why the app offers it only on rest days.

Late workouts and the 3 AM day end

Your day ends at 3:00 AM in your time zone, not at midnight. A session that starts at 11:30 PM and ends after midnight still counts for the day it started.

What if you skip a gym day?

Then the streak resets and your pixel twin gets tired. Log a workout of 10 minutes or more within the next 3 days and it recovers at the same level. If not, it shrinks back one level.

Need a break you didn't plan? A streak freeze from the Shop, bought with gems you earn by training, covers a missed day.

Why rest days should count

Recovery is part of training. A tracker that treats rest as failure pushes you toward skipping recovery or quitting the streak. Counting planned rest keeps the streak honest: it measures whether you followed your plan.

Questions

What counts as a rest day in Gym Twin?

Any day that isn't in your training plan. You can change the plan in Settings and the new plan starts the next day.

Can I work out on a rest day?

Yes. A workout on a rest day keeps the day too.

Does a rest check-in keep a gym day?

No. A gym day needs a workout of 10 minutes or more.
